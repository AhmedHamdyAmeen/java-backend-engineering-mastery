# SRA-013 · Decision-making under uncertainty — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 assumptions ranked · O2 cheap experiments · O3 option value and commitment · O4 decision records.

**Q01 (O1)** Which assumptions should be tested first? A. Low impact, low uncertainty B. Low impact, high uncertainty C. High impact, low uncertainty D. High impact, high uncertainty
**Q02 (O1)** A team lists only technical assumptions for the marketplace. The main gap is: A. Business assumptions, which often carry the highest impact B. Too many diagrams C. Missing code D. No Java version
**Q03 (O1)** What question surfaces assumptions best? A. Who is to blame? B. What must be true for this plan to work? C. When is the deadline? D. How many engineers?
**Q04 (O2)** A good experiment defines in advance: A. A press release B. The tool brand C. What decision each outcome causes D. A name for the team
**Q05 (O2)** An experiment whose result cannot change the decision is: A. Useful for morale B. Required C. A guaranteed success D. Waste
**Q06 (O2)** Cheapest way to test whether sellers will use an API? A. Build the full platform B. Pilot with three sellers using documentation only C. Survey executives D. Wait a year
**Q07 (O3)** When is keeping an option open most justified? A. Uncertainty is high and learning is coming soon B. Facts are all known C. Delay costs more than the option D. The contract is signed
**Q08 (O3)** A thin interface in front of the payment provider mainly: A. Removes testing B. Raises latency C. Keeps a second provider available cheaply D. Replaces the contract
**Q09 (O4)** Why record assumptions in a decision record? A. To lengthen documents B. To show who decided C. To avoid reviews D. To know which decisions to revisit when one fails
**Q10 (O4)** A key assumption fails after half the work is built. The sound approach is: A. Continue, since the work is done B. Decide on remaining cost and future benefit, ignoring sunk cost C. Hide the signal D. Restart everything

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 A · Q03 B · Q04 C · Q05 D · Q06 B · Q07 A · Q08 C · Q09 D · Q10 B

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-013-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab with a mid-lab invalidated assumption (decision simulation). Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a simulation where the response follows from the recorded assumptions and decisions are revisited with reasons.
