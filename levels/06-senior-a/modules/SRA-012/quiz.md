# SRA-012 · Engineering standards — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 what to standardize · O2 principles, defaults, exceptions · O3 adoption through paved roads · O4 retiring standards.

**Q01 (O1)** A director wants every service in one language first. Which reply is best? A. Agree, it is easy B. Ask for a vote C. Offer boundary standards first, since cross-team pain is rarely language D. Postpone all standards
**Q02 (O1)** Which is the best candidate to standardize? A. Internal package layout B. The event envelope shared by many consumers C. Test naming D. Choice of mapper library
**Q03 (O1)** Why is inconsistency at a boundary costly? A. It hurts teams other than the owner and operations B. It only affects style C. It slows compilers D. It affects only the author
**Q04 (O2)** A standard written as principle, default, and exceptions has the advantage that: A. It is shorter than any mandate B. It needs no tooling C. It forbids deviation D. It is firm on the intent and flexible on the means
**Q05 (O2)** Which field is least needed in an exception record? A. Expiry or review date B. Owner C. Risk accepted D. The requester's seniority
**Q06 (O2)** A standard with no exception path will most likely: A. Be broken quietly by the first hard case B. Be followed perfectly C. Need no review D. Reduce risk
**Q07 (O3)** The best way to get voluntary adoption is: A. A deadline email B. A paved road that is the easiest path C. Review gates only D. Public blame
**Q08 (O3)** What shows adoption is working? A. Few complaints B. A signed memo C. Percent of services conforming per team, tracked over time D. Many meetings
**Q09 (O4)** A standard has 40% exceptions sharing one cause. Best action? A. Add more enforcement B. Ignore it C. Retire all standards D. Simplify it or change the default to address the cause
**Q10 (O4)** Why retire standards actively? A. To remove the checks and docs so remaining standards keep their credibility B. To reduce headcount C. To avoid tooling D. To shorten meetings only

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 A · Q04 D · Q05 D · Q06 A · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 D · Q10 A

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-012-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab with three standards and one processed exception. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a standards review that shows measurable adoption, a paved road per standard, and a documented exception.
