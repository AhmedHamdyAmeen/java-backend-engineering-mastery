# SRA-001 · Architecture at scale — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 platforms, domains, capabilities · O2 platform boundaries · O3 evolutionary architecture · O4 organization and architecture misalignment.

**Q01 (O1)** A shared library used by ten teams with no owner or roadmap is best called: A. A platform B. Shared coupling C. A product domain D. A fitness function
**Q02 (O1)** Which test best separates a platform from a shared capability? A. Number of servers B. Language used C. Self-service use by product teams, run as a product with an owner D. Age of the code
**Q03 (O1)** Seller onboarding with its own goals and metrics is a: A. Product domain B. Shared library C. Platform D. Constraint
**Q04 (O2)** When should a capability become a platform? A. When it is new and unstable B. When several teams repeat a stable need C. When leadership likes the word D. Never; teams should own everything
**Q05 (O2)** A good boundary has: A. Shared database tables B. Many owners C. A stable interface and a team that can change the inside alone D. A mandate to migrate by a date
**Q06 (O3)** A fitness function is: A. A measure of developer fitness B. An automated check that an architectural quality still holds C. A load test only D. A design review meeting
**Q07 (O3)** "Last responsible moment" means: A. As late as possible B. Never decide C. Decide first D. Delay a decision until delaying costs more than deciding
**Q08 (O3)** For a likely-to-change choice such as a payout provider, a good approach is to: A. Hard-code the provider B. Keep the choice open behind an interface C. Ignore it D. Build every option now
**Q09 (O4)** The strongest evidence of team and system misalignment is: A. Change history showing many teams editing one service B. A survey of favorite tools C. Number of services D. Team size alone
**Q10 (O4)** A system with no clear owner is: A. Safe B. A platform C. Likely to decay first, an ownership gap D. Fully automated

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 A · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 B · Q07 D · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-001-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, defended as a landscape map to role-played team leads. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a landscape map whose five hot spots the reviewers accept as real.
