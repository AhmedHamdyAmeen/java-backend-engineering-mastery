# SRA-015 · Cross-functional leadership — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRA-015-LAB1 · Marketplace launch plan
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~2 h
1. Write a goals, constraints, and measures profile for product, finance, security, and legal partners.
2. Build a joint launch plan with scope tiers, dates, costs, and risks, with trade-offs visible.
3. Run a planning session with role-played partners and negotiate scope, date, and risk, including one late requirement.
4. Record decisions and owners, and write a relationship plan for each partner.
**Acceptance:** each partner signs off on the trade-offs that affect them.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRA-015-EX01 | 1 | Write what finance, security, and legal each need from engineering for the marketplace launch, and in what form. |
| SRA-015-EX02 | 2 | Convert an engineering plan into a joint plan with scope tiers and visible trade-offs. |
| SRA-015-EX03 | 3 | Product demands "launch 1 March, full scope". List interests behind it and three options. |
| SRA-015-EX04 | 3 | Prepare a negotiation with security about a late identity-verification requirement. |
| SRA-015-EX05 | 4 | Write a relationship plan for one partner with cadence, what you give, and what you learn. |
| SRA-015-EX06 | 2 | Review an engineering status that hides a risk from product and rewrite it to share the risk and ask for a decision. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Finance: cost drivers and forecast ranges; security: data flows, threat model, evidence of controls; legal: regulatory scope per country and contract terms; deliver in a quarterly one-pager and ad hoc for changes.
- **EX02** Tiers: must (seller onboarding, payouts), should (reporting), could (promotions); each tier has date, cost, and owner; the choices are product's and finance's with you.
- **EX03** Interests: seller commitment, competitor timing, revenue plan. Options: reduced scope on 1 March, full scope on a later date, full scope with added people (cost and ramp-up risk).
- **EX04** Understand the minimum control; offer phased control (limits on new-seller payouts, manual review), date for the full control, risk owner; avoid escalating over security.
- **EX05** Example: monthly 30-minute check with finance, shared forecast, early notice of cost changes; learn their planning calendar and constraints.
- **EX06** State the risk, its likelihood and impact, options (scope cut, date move, mitigation), and the decision needed with a date.

Outcomes 1 and 4 are also covered by the lab profiles and relationship plans.

**Workplace evidence:** a cross-functional plan you co-led.
