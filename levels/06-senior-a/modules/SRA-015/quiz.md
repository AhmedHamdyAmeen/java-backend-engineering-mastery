# SRA-015 · Cross-functional leadership — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 partner goals and constraints · O2 joint plans · O3 negotiating as peers · O4 durable relationships.

**Q01 (O1)** Why is treating security as a blocker counterproductive? A. It costs money B. It is slower C. It ignores their legitimate goals and makes late fights likelier D. It is illegal
**Q02 (O1)** What does finance most need from engineering? A. Source code B. Cost drivers and forecast ranges with assumptions C. Architecture diagrams D. Test coverage
**Q03 (O1)** All partners most want from engineering: A. Fast code B. Fewer meetings C. Large teams D. Honest, early information on options, cost, and risk
**Q04 (O2)** Joint planning differs from handing over a plan because: A. Partners see and own the trade-offs from the start B. It is faster C. It avoids estimates D. It removes risk
**Q05 (O2)** Which makes a trade-off visible? A. A longer plan B. Scope tiers with dates and costs where each choice has an owner C. A Gantt chart only D. Silence
**Q06 (O2)** Product's status shows "green" while you know of a serious risk. You should: A. Keep it green B. Wait until it happens C. Share the risk with options and a decision date D. Escalate privately only
**Q07 (O3)** Product demands "1 March, full scope". First move: A. Refuse B. Accept C. Escalate D. Find the interest behind the demand and offer options
**Q08 (O3)** Security adds a late requirement. Best approach: A. Go to the executive to override B. Agree a phased control with a date for the full control and a risk owner C. Ignore it D. Delay the launch silently
**Q09 (O4)** Trust across functions is built mainly by: A. Reliability and early, honest updates before crises B. Titles C. Escalations D. Formal memos only
**Q10 (O4)** A joint success should be: A. Claimed by engineering B. Left unmentioned C. Credited publicly to the partners involved D. Reported privately

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 D · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-015-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab (negotiation simulation with role-played partners). Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a simulation in which risk is stated honestly, options are offered, and each partner signs off on the trade-offs affecting them.
