# SRA-019 · Executive communication — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 risk as business impact · O2 the executive brief · O3 hard questions and uncertainty · O4 securing a decision.

**Q01 (O1)** Which statement best conveys a database risk to a CEO? A. "The failover is untested" B. "We lack replication lag alarms" C. "A database failure at peak could stop checkout for up to two hours; roughly X to Y revenue per hour at risk (assumption stated)" D. "The database is old"
**Q02 (O1)** To make impact credible you should: A. Give a single exact number B. Use adjectives C. Avoid numbers D. Give ranges with stated assumptions
**Q03 (O1)** Showing the cost of doing nothing alongside the cost of acting helps because: A. The executive can compare the two choices B. It lengthens the brief C. It reduces cost D. It avoids questions
**Q04 (O2)** The correct structure of an executive brief is: A. Background, history, details, ask B. Situation, recommendation, options, ask C. Architecture, design, risks D. Options, history, summary
**Q05 (O2)** A good ask contains: A. A general wish B. A deadline only C. A specific decision, amount, and date D. A list of tasks
**Q06 (O2)** The pitch starts with: A. The architecture B. The history C. Team structure D. The situation and the ask
**Q07 (O3)** You do not know the answer to a question. You should: A. Guess B. Say so, and say how and by when you will find out C. Deflect D. Promise certainty
**Q08 (O3)** A good answer to a hard question is: A. One sentence first, then the reason B. A long technical explanation C. A counter-question D. Silence
**Q09 (O4)** Pre-wiring executives before the meeting does what? A. Replaces the meeting B. Lets you avoid discussion C. Resolves objections beforehand so the meeting can decide D. Hides the ask
**Q10 (O4)** After the decision, you should: A. Wait B. Confirm decision, owner, and next steps in writing the same day C. Celebrate D. Begin new asks

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 D · Q03 A · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 D · Q07 B · Q08 A · Q09 C · Q10 B

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-019-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab pitch. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a pitch scored on the COM and LEA dimensions in which a clear decision is reached within the slot, risks are stated as business impact, and uncertainty is acknowledged.
