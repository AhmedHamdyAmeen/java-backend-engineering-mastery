# SRA-019 · Executive communication — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRA-019-LAB1 · Funding pitch
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~2 h
1. Write the ask for year one of Commerce 2030, with the outcome it buys.
2. Translate the top three technical risks into business impact with ranges and assumptions.
3. Write the executive brief (situation, recommendation, options, ask) and one page of backup.
4. Deliver a 10-minute pitch to role-played executives, handle hard questions, and admit uncertainty where it exists.
5. Confirm the decision in writing.
**Acceptance:** a clear decision is reached within the slot.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRA-019-EX01 | 1 | Translate five technical risks (for example untested failover, unowned data, end-of-life services) into business impact with ranges. |
| SRA-019-EX02 | 2 | Rewrite a technical status email into a one-page executive brief. |
| SRA-019-EX03 | 2 | Write the ask for a resilience investment with amount, decision date, and cost of delay. |
| SRA-019-EX04 | 3 | Prepare answers to five hard questions (why now, why this much, what if we wait, who is to blame, how sure are you). |
| SRA-019-EX05 | 4 | Plan the pre-wire for a funding meeting: whom to meet, what to ask each, and how objections change the pitch. |
| SRA-019-EX06 | 3 | Draft the CEO outage brief with known facts, unknowns, actions, and the next update time. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Example: untested failover becomes "up to two hours of checkout outage in a peak event; revenue at risk is X to Y per hour (assumption: orders per hour)"; end-of-life becomes support cost and security exposure by date.
- **EX02** Situation in three lines with the key number, recommendation, two or three options, ask; technical detail moved to backup.
- **EX03** "Approve 2.1 million for year one by 15 March; delay beyond that postpones the marketplace launch by a quarter" (figures illustrative).
- **EX04** One-sentence answers with supporting fact; for "how sure" give a range and what would change it; do not assign blame, describe the cause and fix.
- **EX05** Meet the finance lead (cost basis), the product lead (launch), and the CTO (priority) before; revise the pitch to answer their objections; the meeting then decides.
- **EX06** Lead with impact and recovery; "cause identified so far: …; unknown: …; next update at 16:00"; actions with cost; one recommendation.

Outcome 2 is also covered by the lab brief; outcome 4 by the lab decision.

**Workplace evidence:** an executive decision or funding you secured.
