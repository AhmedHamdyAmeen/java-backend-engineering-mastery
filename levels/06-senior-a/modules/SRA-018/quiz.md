# SRA-018 · Organizational bottlenecks — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 Conway's Law and inverse Conway · O2 measuring coordination cost · O3 team boundary changes with managers · O4 cognitive load and team topologies.

**Q01 (O1)** Two teams must agree on every release and share a table. According to Conway's Law, the result is: A. A clean boundary B. Tight coupling at that seam mirroring their communication C. Faster delivery D. A bug
**Q02 (O1)** The inverse Conway maneuver means: A. Reorganizing randomly B. Reversing deployments C. Designing teams to match the target architecture D. Merging all teams
**Q03 (O1)** Reorganizing teams without changing the architecture usually: A. Solves coupling B. Reduces cost C. Improves ownership D. Leaves the old coupling in place
**Q04 (O2)** Which best measures coordination cost? A. Lines of code B. Share of changes needing another team and wait time C. Number of services D. Meeting count
**Q05 (O2)** The largest queue belongs to the platform team. A better finding than "they are slow" is: A. They are lazy B. They need a bigger room C. They own many unrelated responsibilities and are overloaded D. Nothing
**Q06 (O2)** Why measure before proposing? A. It looks professional B. It is required C. To lengthen the process D. Data moves the debate from opinions to evidence
**Q07 (O3)** In a team design conversation, managers own: A. The target architecture B. The dependency data C. Structure, hiring, and people decisions D. Code reviews
**Q08 (O3)** Which proposal is strongest? A. Reorganization chart only B. A split with interfaces, data ownership, transition steps, and predicted dependency reduction C. A new team name D. A hiring request
**Q09 (O4)** Team Topologies lists these team types: A. Frontend, backend, data, QA B. Junior, mid, senior, lead C. Stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated-subsystem D. Product, design, sales, support
**Q10 (O4)** A team with four domains, nine services, and heavy on-call shows: A. Spare capacity B. Cognitive load above its limit C. Good ownership D. Strong autonomy

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 D · Q07 C · Q08 B · Q09 C · Q10 B

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-018-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab proposal presented to role-played managers. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a proposal review showing measured dependencies, alignment to the context map, manager constraints respected, and a predicted reduction in cross-team dependencies.
