# SRA-021 · AI strategy and build vs buy — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 finding AI value · O2 hosted, open-weight, managed · O3 prompting, RAG, fine-tuning · O4 AI cost, lock-in, data foundations.

**Q01 (O1)** A strong AI proposal starts from: A. A model demo B. A vendor pitch C. A competitor's feature D. A business problem with a measure of value
**Q02 (O1)** A poor fit for AI is a task with: A. Fuzzy language input B. Exact rules and zero error tolerance C. Draft output reviewed by people D. Fuzzy ranking signals
**Q03 (O1)** Marketplace leaders ask for a list of AI demos. The better artifact is: A. A longer list B. A press release C. A prioritized portfolio with value, risk, cost, and at least one rejection D. A model comparison only
**Q04 (O2)** Which option gives the most control over data at scale but the most operating burden? A. Hosted API B. Open-weight self-hosted C. Managed platform D. No model
**Q05 (O2)** Which basis best compares models for your use case? A. Your own evaluation set on your task B. Public leaderboard only C. Marketing claims D. Model size
**Q06 (O3)** Which technique suits frequently changing private documents? A. Fine-tuning always B. Training from scratch C. Retrieval-augmented generation D. Nothing
**Q07 (O3)** The usual escalation order when quality is short is: A. Fine-tune, prompt, retrieve B. Retrieve, fine-tune, prompt C. Prompt, retrieve, then consider fine-tuning D. Fine-tune only
**Q08 (O4)** Which cost is easy to miss when self-hosting a model? A. Engineers, idle GPU capacity, redundancy, and upgrades B. Electricity only C. Nothing D. Logos
**Q09 (O4)** Lock-in is reduced by: A. Prompts coded per provider B. Longer contracts C. An abstraction layer, evaluation suite, and tested second option D. Fewer tests
**Q10 (O4)** Why do data foundations matter for AI? A. They do not B. Poor or unowned data yields wrong answers in retrieval and fine-tuning C. Data is cheap D. Models fix any data

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 B · Q05 A · Q06 C · Q07 C · Q08 A · Q09 C · Q10 B

Six case exercises `SRA-021-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, defended before a role-played CTO and CFO. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a strategy with at least one idea rejected with reasons and costs traceable to usage assumptions.
