# SRA-022 · Responsible AI and AI compliance — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 responsible-AI controls · O2 privacy and residency of model calls · O3 risk-based classification · O4 AI auditability.

**Q01 (O1)** A responsible-AI principle becomes engineering when it has: A. A poster B. A control, an owner, and a test C. A meeting D. A release date
**Q02 (O1)** Human oversight that only clicks "approve" is: A. Ideal B. Required C. Rubber stamping and weak oversight D. Cheaper and equal
**Q03 (O1)** For decisions that materially affect people you need: A. No notice B. A bigger model C. Faster responses D. An appeal path with a human decision
**Q04 (O2)** Before sending personal data to a hosted model, first check: A. Provider terms on retention, location, and training use B. The logo C. Token price only D. Nothing
**Q05 (O2)** A privacy-friendly practice for model inputs is to: A. Send everything B. Send only what the task needs and redact identifiers C. Disable logs D. Share the prompt publicly
**Q06 (O3)** A risk-based approach means obligations: A. Are identical for all uses B. Depend on the technology brand C. Rise with the potential harm of the use D. Apply only to large companies
**Q07 (O3)** The correct basis to classify a use case is: A. The model type B. Its effect on people C. The team's size D. The programming language
**Q08 (O3)** Who confirms the risk class and obligations? A. The model B. A random reviewer C. The vendor D. Legal and compliance partners
**Q09 (O4)** Why record model and prompt versions in audit records? A. They are small B. They are required for billing C. Behavior changes by version, so reconstruction needs exact versions D. Prompts are secret
**Q10 (O4)** Which is the best use of AI audit records? A. Marketing B. Incident review, appeals, and regulator requests C. Deleting data D. Training without consent

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 B · Q08 D · Q09 C · Q10 B

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-022-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, reviewed as a controls package by a role-played legal and compliance advisor. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a package in which every high-risk use has human oversight and an audit trail.
