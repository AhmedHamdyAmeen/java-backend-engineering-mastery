# SRA-006 · Enterprise resilience — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 regional and provider failure · O2 chaos engineering programs · O3 systemic risk · O4 presenting resilience posture.

**Q01 (O1)** In a regional failure plan, which is often forgotten? A. The logo B. Colors C. Test data D. Identity and deployment tooling needed to recover
**Q02 (O1)** Which statement about failover plans is correct? A. An untested plan is a hope B. Documents are enough C. Active-active is always required D. Only cost matters
**Q03 (O1)** Which should survive a regional failure first? A. Internal wiki B. Checkout and payments C. Marketing banners D. Recommendation tuning
**Q04 (O2)** A chaos experiment must include: A. Only a tool B. A surprise C. A hypothesis, blast-radius control, and abort criteria D. Maximum traffic
**Q05 (O2)** The best place to start an experiment: A. Pre-production or a small cohort B. Production at full scale C. During peak sales D. Without telling anyone
**Q06 (O2)** After an experiment you should: A. Delete the results B. Run another immediately C. Skip review D. Review learnings, assign owners, and schedule follow-up
**Q07 (O3)** Three unrelated incidents all involve an expiring internal certificate. This is: A. Coincidence B. Bad luck C. A correlated systemic risk to be owned and mitigated D. A single-team fault
**Q08 (O3)** A single engineer everything depends on is: A. A strength B. A systemic risk C. A fitness function D. A chaos tool
**Q09 (O4)** Leadership best understands a risk expressed as: A. Business impact such as revenue lost per hour B. A CPU graph C. A log excerpt D. A code diff
**Q10 (O4)** A resilience investment list should include: A. Only the largest items B. Only new tools C. Ranked options with cost, risk reduced, and the option to accept a risk D. Only chaos tests

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 A · Q03 B · Q04 C · Q05 A · Q06 D · Q07 C · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-006-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, presented as a resilience posture to role-played leadership. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a posture whose top risk is quantified in business impact.
