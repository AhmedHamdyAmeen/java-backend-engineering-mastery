# SRA-014 · Leading ambiguous initiatives — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRA-014-LAB1 · Fragile checkout
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~2 h
1. From provided interview notes and incident data, find patterns and write a problem statement (who, what, evidence, impact, scope, success measure).
2. Meet role-played stakeholders (product, SRE, support, engineering manager) individually, then hold a short alignment session.
3. Record agreement on the problem in writing.
4. Plan the first two milestones with value, risk, and measures.
5. Draft the handoff note naming the receiving team and your exit date.
**Acceptance:** stakeholders agree on the problem statement in writing.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRA-014-EX01 | 1 | Given five raw signals about "slow to change", write a problem statement with evidence and no solution. |
| SRA-014-EX02 | 1 | Edit a draft statement that contains "we need a new platform" into a clean problem statement. |
| SRA-014-EX03 | 2 | Plan the alignment conversations: order of stakeholders, what each must confirm, and how you handle a disagreement. |
| SRA-014-EX04 | 3 | Choose and justify the first two milestones for fragile checkout using early value and riskiest-first. |
| SRA-014-EX05 | 4 | Write a handoff note for the receiving team, including decision rights and your office hours. |
| SRA-014-EX06 | 4 | Decide whether to hand off now or in two months given three facts about the team's capacity and the problem clarity. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Example: median change waits six days for approval; two teams hold approvals; support tickets and incidents cluster around the same releases; impact stated in lead time and failure rate.
- **EX02** Replace the solution with the observed delay and its measured effect; list solutions separately for later discussion.
- **EX03** Meet individuals first, then the group; each confirms problem, impact estimate, scope; where wording differs, reconcile in writing; escalate only unresolved scope questions.
- **EX04** M1: six weeks, timeouts and dashboard on the top two failure points (visible value, quick learning); M2: isolate payment calls, measure checkout failure rate; riskiest assumption tested in M1.
- **EX05** Contents: problem statement, alignment record, milestones, risks, who decides what, contact period until a set date.
- **EX06** Hand off when the problem is clear and the team has mandate and capacity; if capacity is missing, secure it or delay the handoff, but set a date.

All four outcomes are also exercised in the lab.

**Workplace evidence:** an initiative you started from an ambiguous problem and handed to a team.
