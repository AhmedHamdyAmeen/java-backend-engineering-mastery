# SRA-014 · Leading ambiguous initiatives — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 defining the problem · O2 alignment before solutions · O3 sequencing for early value · O4 handing off.

**Q01 (O1)** "Checkout feels fragile" arrives as a signal. Your first step is: A. Pick an architecture B. Gather evidence and find patterns C. Rewrite checkout D. Ask for headcount
**Q02 (O1)** Which element does a good problem statement not contain? A. Who is hurt B. Evidence C. A preferred solution D. Scope
**Q03 (O1)** "Median change waits six days for approval" is: A. Evidence that gives the problem a measure B. A solution C. A roadmap D. An opinion
**Q04 (O2)** Why meet stakeholders one at a time before the group session? A. To save time B. To avoid surprises and learn each wording C. To avoid writing anything D. To decide the solution
**Q05 (O2)** Stakeholders argue over architectures. The likely cause is: A. Poor tooling B. Too few engineers C. They were never aligned on the problem D. Bad meeting rooms
**Q06 (O2)** What marks alignment as complete? A. Applause B. A long meeting C. A signed solution D. Written agreement on problem, impact, and scope
**Q07 (O3)** Which first milestone is best? A. A quarter-long platform rewrite B. A six-week change removing real pain on the top two failure points C. Documentation only D. A reorganization
**Q08 (O3)** Why do the riskiest part early? A. It is the easiest B. Management prefers it C. It impresses sponsors D. You learn cheaply whether the plan works
**Q09 (O4)** You have owned an initiative for a year and the team is ready. You should: A. Keep it for control B. Hand over why, evidence, decisions, and decision rights, then step back C. Disappear without notes D. Add new scope
**Q10 (O4)** A handoff note should include: A. Only the code location B. Only the budget C. Problem statement, alignment record, milestones, risks, and who decides what D. Personal opinions only

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 A · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 D · Q07 B · Q08 D · Q09 B · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-014-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab (initiative brief and alignment session). Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a brief and session in which stakeholders agree the problem statement in writing and the first two milestones are sequenced for early value.
