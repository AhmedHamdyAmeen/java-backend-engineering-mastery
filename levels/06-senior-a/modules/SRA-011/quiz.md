# SRA-011 · Technical vision — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 writing a concrete vision · O2 vision, strategy, roadmap · O3 formats for audiences · O4 feedback and revision.

**Q01 (O1)** Two Commerce teams chose different messaging stacks in the same month. What would a good vision have done? A. Settled the recurring question in advance with a principle B. Banned both C. Replaced the teams D. Fixed the roadmap
**Q02 (O1)** Which statement is concrete enough to guide decisions? A. "We will be world-class" B. "A new seller goes live in one day with no engineering ticket" C. "Quality matters" D. "We embrace modern technology"
**Q03 (O1)** A vision that costs nothing guides nothing. What should it therefore include? A. More adjectives B. A budget C. A list of what we will not do D. A staffing table
**Q04 (O2)** "Q1 onboarding service, Q3 settlement" is a: A. Vision B. Strategy C. Principle D. Roadmap
**Q05 (O2)** "Own the seller domain, buy payments" is a: A. Strategy B. Vision C. Roadmap D. Status report
**Q06 (O2)** A draft vision lists ten projects with dates. The main problem is: A. It is too short B. It is a roadmap in disguise C. It lacks a logo D. It is too ambitious
**Q07 (O3)** The CFO has five minutes. The best format is: A. The full document B. Architecture diagrams C. Code samples D. A three-bullet outcome summary with cost and revenue effect
**Q08 (O3)** In the all-hands talk, the engineers ask what changes for them. Your best response is: A. Give one concrete example from a team's current quarter B. Repeat the destination C. Promise no reorganization D. Point to the roadmap
**Q09 (O4)** A team lead says "I prefer the other messaging tool". This is: A. A fact correction B. A scope addition C. A preference to answer with a reason D. A reason to change direction
**Q10 (O4)** Which feedback should change the direction of the vision? A. Loud objections B. A request to add a favorite project C. Evidence that a key assumption is false D. A second opinion from the loudest team

## Answers
Q01 A · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 D · Q05 A · Q06 B · Q07 D · Q08 A · Q09 C · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-011-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab with the talk and feedback log. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a vision document and talk that pass the review (concrete statements, trade-off principles, feedback decisions recorded, team leads can state their next-quarter impact).
