# SRA-011 · Technical vision — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRA-011-LAB1 · Commerce 2030 vision
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~2 h
1. Write the vision document for Commerce 2030: five concrete future-state statements, trade-off principles, a "we will not" list.
2. Produce a one-page summary for team leads.
3. Prepare and give a 10-minute talk to role-played team leads.
4. Collect their feedback in a log, classify each item, and revise the document with a recorded reason for each decision.
5. Ask each team lead to explain how the vision changes their next quarter.
**Acceptance:** team leads can explain how the vision affects their next quarter.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRA-011-EX01 | 1 | Rewrite the statement "Commerce will be modern, scalable, and resilient" into three concrete future-state statements. |
| SRA-011-EX02 | 2 | Sort twelve statements from a draft into vision, strategy, and roadmap, and name which parts are a roadmap in disguise. |
| SRA-011-EX03 | 1 | Apply the decision test: pick two pending Commerce decisions and show how your vision decides each, or fix it if it cannot. |
| SRA-011-EX04 | 3 | Write a one-page summary of the vision for engineering managers. |
| SRA-011-EX05 | 3 | Outline the same vision as a story for engineers and as three bullets for the CFO. |
| SRA-011-EX06 | 4 | Classify eight feedback comments (fact, clarity, preference, scope) and decide accept, adjust, or decline with a reply to each. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Example: "a new seller goes live in one day without engineering tickets"; "any team can ship to any region through one pipeline"; "an incident in one domain never stops checkout".
- **EX02** Destination statements are vision; "own seller domain, buy payments" is strategy; named projects with quarters are roadmap. A vision listing projects and dates is a roadmap in disguise.
- **EX03** Example: "build or adopt a search engine?" The vision says differentiators are built and commodities adopted; search for the marketplace is a differentiator, so build on an adopted core. If it cannot decide, add a principle.
- **EX04** One page: destination, why now, three decisions it changes, what changes in your team this quarter, where to give feedback.
- **EX05** Engineers: a day-in-the-life of a seller launch before and after. CFO: lower cost per order, faster seller onboarding revenue, reduced outage exposure.
- **EX06** Factual correction: accept. Unclear wording: adjust. Preference for a favorite tool: decline with reason. Scope addition: log for the strategy, not the vision. Every comment gets a reply.

Outcome 4 is also covered by the lab feedback log; outcome 2 by the quiz.

**Workplace evidence:** a vision you wrote that teams referenced in their own planning.
