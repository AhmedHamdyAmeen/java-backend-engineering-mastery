# SRA-010 · Technical strategy — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRA-010-LAB1 · Commerce 2030 strategy draft
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3 h
1. Collect evidence from your earlier work: landscape map, roadmap and obsolescence calendar, data ownership, regional plan, resilience posture, forecast, economics, compliance.
2. Write the diagnosis: three to four challenges with causes and evidence.
3. Write three to five guiding policies with explicit trade-offs.
4. Define coherent actions with owners and sequence, and the investment split across three years, including a modernization priority list.
5. Define outcome measures with baselines and targets and a review process.
6. Test traceability: every action to the diagnosis, every measure to an outcome.
**Acceptance:** every action traceable to the diagnosis; outcomes measurable.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRA-010-EX01 | 1 | Critique a "strategy" that lists five goals; rewrite it as diagnosis, policies, and actions. |
| SRA-010-EX02 | 1 | Write a diagnosis of three challenges for a given evidence pack and state what each predicts about policy. |
| SRA-010-EX03 | 2 | Write the allocation for year one (product, platform, reliability) and explain what happens if product asks for 100% product work. |
| SRA-010-EX04 | 2 | Prepare a five-minute defense of a 30% platform allocation including answers to two objections. |
| SRA-010-EX05 | 3 | Prioritize eight modernization candidates by risk, opportunity, cost, and dependency; mark one "never". |
| SRA-010-EX06 | 4 | Define five outcome measures with baselines, targets, owners, and a trigger for revising the strategy. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Goals like "be scalable" have no cause or trade-off. A strategy states: diagnosis (why scaling is hard now), policies (for example regional ownership of state, buy generics), actions (owned migrations), and what is excluded.
- **EX02** Each challenge has cause, evidence, and consequence; the diagnosis should let a reader predict the policy (for example coupling causes delay, so policy limits cross-domain coupling).
- **EX03** For example year one 60% product, 30% platform and modernization, 10% reliability and compliance. At 100% product: lead time and incident risk rise, end-of-life work becomes emergencies; offer a staged compromise tied to measures.
- **EX04** Lead with business outcomes (lead time, risk, cost per order), show evidence, state what 30% buys by milestone, propose a review at six months with fallback.
- **EX05** Rank end-of-life datastores with near deadlines and marketplace enablers first; defer large rewrites with low risk; "never" for a stable system with no change demand and low risk.
- **EX06** Examples: lead time for change, incident minutes, cost per order, share of services past end of support, platform adoption. Revise on missed targets two periods running or changed assumptions.

Outcome 1 is also covered by the lab; outcome 4 by the lab traceability test and quiz.

**Workplace evidence:** a technical strategy you authored or co-authored that guided investment.
