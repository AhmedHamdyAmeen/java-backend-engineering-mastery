# SRA-010 · Technical strategy — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 diagnosis, policies, actions · O2 platform versus product allocation · O3 prioritizing modernization · O4 measuring and revising.

**Q01 (O1)** The three parts of a strategy are: A. Goals, budget, dates B. Vision, mission, values C. Diagnosis, guiding policies, coherent actions D. Plan, build, run
**Q02 (O1)** A list of goals such as "be scalable" is weak because it: A. Has no cause, trade-off, or policy B. Is too specific C. Is too long D. Is too measurable
**Q03 (O1)** A useful guiding policy: A. Says yes to everything B. Avoids trade-offs C. Is a slogan D. Can rule out options and states its trade-off
**Q04 (O1)** A diagnosis should separate causes from: A. Symptoms B. Budgets C. Teams D. Evidence
**Q05 (O2)** An explicit allocation of capacity is needed because: A. Finance asked B. Urgent product work otherwise absorbs all capacity C. It is a rule D. Teams are large
**Q06 (O2)** The best way to defend a platform allocation to product leaders: A. Argue code purity B. Threaten delays C. Wait D. Argue business outcomes with delivery and risk evidence and commit to measures
**Q07 (O3)** Modernization is best prioritized by: A. Age of system B. Business risk, opportunity, cost, and dependency C. Developer preference D. Alphabetical order
**Q08 (O3)** A stable old system with no change demand and low risk should: A. Be rewritten first B. Be ignored forever with no review C. Possibly be left alone, explicitly decided D. Be deleted
**Q09 (O4)** Outcome measures need: A. Baselines, targets, owners, and a reporting rhythm B. Only dashboards C. Colors D. Annual surprises
**Q10 (O4)** A strategy should be revised: A. Never B. On regular review and when key assumptions change C. Only after failure D. Every day

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 A · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 D · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 A · Q10 B

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-010-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, defended as a strategy to role-played product leadership. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a strategy draft in which every action traces to the diagnosis and outcomes are measurable.
