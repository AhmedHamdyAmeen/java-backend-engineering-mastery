# SRA-016 · Engineering capability development — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRA-016-LAB1 · Capability plan
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~2 h
1. List the capabilities Commerce 2030's strategy requires at 12 and 36 months (for example event-driven design, SRE practices).
2. Assess provided data for eight teams and build a gap heat map with evidence.
3. Design a one-year development plan using more than one lever (mentorship system, learning path, a community of practice, tooling, hiring).
4. Define measures, baselines, and review dates.
5. Map each gap to a strategy action.
**Acceptance:** plan ties gaps to strategy actions.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRA-016-EX01 | 1 | From the strategy, list five capabilities needed in 12 months and state the evidence you would collect for each. |
| SRA-016-EX02 | 1 | Given incident data for four teams, rate their SRE practice and justify each rating. |
| SRA-016-EX03 | 2 | Design a learning path and mentor system for event-driven design across 25 teams. |
| SRA-016-EX04 | 3 | Write a charter for a reliability guild with leadership rotation and monthly outputs. |
| SRA-016-EX05 | 3 | A community launched six months ago has faded. Diagnose and propose a recovery. |
| SRA-016-EX06 | 4 | Define a scorecard that tells whether capability development is working, with baseline and target. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Event design (design review outcomes), SRE practice (incident reviews, on-call data), data contracts (consumer breakage count), observability (trace coverage), security practice (findings trend).
- **EX02** Ratings follow evidence: repeated timeouts missing means "learning"; tested runbooks and alert quality mean "practicing"; avoid ranking individuals.
- **EX03** Reading, shadowing a design review, pairing on a real flow, then leading a small design; mentors trained and matched to gaps; office hours; track who is reached.
- **EX04** Purpose, three leaders, monthly cadence with an output (checklist, postmortem digest), sponsor, rotation every six months, measure adoption and incident rate.
- **EX05** Likely causes: no output, no sponsor, a single leader who left; restart with a real incident, new co-leaders, and a visible deliverable.
- **EX06** Outcomes: mean time to recover, repeat incident rate, share of teams with tested runbooks; leading: engineers able to lead a design; baseline from the first assessment, reviewed quarterly.

Outcome 2 is also covered by the lab plan; outcome 4 by the lab measures.

**Workplace evidence:** a community of practice or learning program you started that outlived your direct involvement.
