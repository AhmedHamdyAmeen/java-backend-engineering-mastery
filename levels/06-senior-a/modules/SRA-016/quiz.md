# SRA-016 · Engineering capability development — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 assessing capability gaps · O2 mentorship systems and learning paths · O3 communities of practice · O4 measuring growth.

**Q01 (O1)** Where should a capability assessment start? A. With individual rankings B. From the capabilities the strategy requires C. With the training catalog D. With hiring plans
**Q02 (O1)** Which gives the best evidence of a team's SRE practice? A. Training attendance B. Years of experience C. Incident reviews and on-call data D. Self-confidence
**Q03 (O1)** The gap analysis finds weak event design in most teams. The best response is to: A. Send everyone to a course B. Fire managers C. Wait D. Choose the right lever per gap (guild, pairing, tooling, training) and sequence them
**Q04 (O2)** Why is one-to-one mentoring alone insufficient? A. It is expensive B. It is disliked C. It does not scale to 25 teams D. It is inaccurate
**Q05 (O2)** A mentor system should also: A. Train mentors and match them to strategy gaps B. Avoid real work C. Remove learning paths D. Ban pairing
**Q06 (O2)** The most effective learning path includes: A. Reading only B. A final exam C. Slides D. Reading, shadowing, pairing on real work, and leading with support
**Q07 (O3)** A new guild should start with: A. A big launch event B. A mailing list C. Real pain, two or three committed leaders, and a monthly output D. A budget
**Q08 (O3)** Which most likely makes a community fade? A. Rotating leaders B. A sponsor C. A cadence D. No purpose or visible output after the launch
**Q09 (O4)** Which measure best shows capability growth in reliability? A. Mean time to recover and repeat incident rate B. Training hours C. Slide count D. Number of meetings
**Q10 (O4)** An activity does not move the outcome measures after two quarters. You should: A. Add more of it B. Ignore the measures C. Stop or change it D. Hide the data

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 C · Q05 A · Q06 D · Q07 C · Q08 D · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-016-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab (plan review). Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a plan review showing gaps backed by evidence, more than one lever, measures with baselines, and each gap tied to a strategy action.
