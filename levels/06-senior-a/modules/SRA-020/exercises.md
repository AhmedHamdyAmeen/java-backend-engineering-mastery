# SRA-020 · Succession and delegation — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRA-020-LAB1 · Make yourself replaceable
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~1.5 h
1. Build a key-person risk map for Commerce 2030, including the learner's own role: decisions, knowledge, and approvals.
2. Choose three critical areas and write a succession plan for each: successor path, scope ladder, and timeline.
3. Write a decision-rights document for each area.
4. Identify knowledge to record and where it will live; plan a test by someone else.
5. Define how you will check, after a quarter, that each area runs without you.
**Acceptance:** each area has a named successor path and decision-rights document.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRA-020-EX01 | 1 | List your top five dependencies (decisions, knowledge, approvals) and what stops if you are absent for a month. |
| SRA-020-EX02 | 2 | Rewrite a task delegation ("update the retention settings") as ownership delegation of the event platform's retention policy. |
| SRA-020-EX03 | 2 | Write a decision-rights table for the event platform (four decision types). |
| SRA-020-EX04 | 3 | Design a successor ladder for the architecture review practice with timing and success criteria. |
| SRA-020-EX05 | 4 | Make a survival checklist for one area and describe how you will test it. |
| SRA-020-EX06 | 3 | A successor keeps asking you to decide. Diagnose and plan how to respond. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Examples: approving cross-domain schema changes, incident escalation for payments, historic knowledge on the billing model; each gets absence impact and time to trouble.
- **EX02** Ownership: the platform team owns retention policy goals (cost, compliance), decides within limits, consults security for personal data, and reports quarterly; not just "change the setting".
- **EX03** Schema change: owner decides, consumers consulted; new topic: owner decides; retention: owner decides, security consulted; breaking cross-domain change: architecture group decides.
- **EX04** Observe, review with you, lead with you reviewing, lead alone, then train another reviewer; success is reviews continuing on time without you.
- **EX05** Decision records, area guide, runbooks, contacts, access; test by having a colleague handle a mock request using only the documents.
- **EX06** Likely unclear decision rights or low confidence; clarify limits, ask for their recommendation first, back their choices within limits, and review outcomes together.

Outcome 1 is also covered by the lab risk map and quiz.

**Workplace evidence:** an area you handed over that continued to thrive without you.
