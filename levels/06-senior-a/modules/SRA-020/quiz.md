# SRA-020 · Succession and delegation — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 key-person risk · O2 delegating ownership and decision rights · O3 growing successors · O4 knowledge that survives.

**Q01 (O1)** Which is a sign of key-person risk? A. Teams decide on their own B. Everything routes through one person for approval C. Runbooks are tested D. Decisions are recorded
**Q02 (O1)** Why is being indispensable a problem? A. It is a single point of failure and limits your own growth B. It raises salary C. It is good for stability D. It is rare
**Q03 (O1)** When mapping your own dependencies you should list: A. Tasks only B. Meetings C. Decisions and knowledge that others rely on you for D. Tools
**Q04 (O2)** What distinguishes ownership from a task? A. Longer duration B. The authority to decide and responsibility for the outcome C. A bigger team D. A deadline
**Q05 (O2)** You hand over the event platform but still approve every schema change. This is: A. Ownership delegation B. Good governance C. Succession D. Task delegation without decision rights
**Q06 (O2)** A decision-rights table names: A. Who decides, who is consulted, who is informed B. Who is senior C. Who is to blame D. Who reports to whom
**Q07 (O3)** The most reliable sign that succession works is: A. A signed plan B. A training course C. The area runs well when you are away D. The successor's title
**Q08 (O3)** A good way to grow a successor is to: A. Give them tasks only B. Give increasing scope from shadowing to leading, with visible moments C. Wait until you leave D. Hire externally
**Q09 (O4)** Which knowledge is most valuable to record? A. The why behind decisions B. Meeting times C. Usernames D. Office layout
**Q10 (O4)** You test your documentation by: A. Rereading it B. Asking for approval C. Having someone else use it to do the job without you D. Counting pages

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 A · Q03 C · Q04 B · Q05 D · Q06 A · Q07 C · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-020-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab (plan review). Pass: quiz at least 8/10, and a plan review in which each of three critical areas has a named successor path and a decision-rights document, and the learner's own role is on the risk map.
