# SRA-008 · Architecture economics — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 build vs buy vs open source and TCO · O2 opportunity and exit cost · O3 real options · O4 finance-checkable cases.

**Q01 (O1)** Comparing a vendor licence with zero for the build option: A. Is correct B. Is required C. Is simple and fair D. Ignores engineering, operation, and compliance costs of building
**Q02 (O1)** Open source software is: A. Free of all cost B. Free of licence fees but costly to operate and upgrade C. Always cheaper D. Always insecure
**Q03 (O1)** A typical TCO horizon for platform decisions: A. One month B. One day C. Several years, such as five D. Never
**Q04 (O1)** The subdomain guidance for sourcing is usually: A. Build the core, buy or adopt generic capabilities B. Build everything C. Buy everything D. Build generics
**Q05 (O2)** Opportunity cost is: A. The licence fee B. The value of the best alternative use of the same resources C. A tax D. A discount
**Q06 (O2)** Exit cost should be included because: A. Vendors dislike it B. It lowers cost C. It shows how locked in the decision is D. It is optional
**Q07 (O3)** A real option is: A. A stock grant B. A feature flag only C. A bug D. The right, not the obligation, to act later at a known cost
**Q08 (O3)** Paying to keep a decision open is most justified when: A. Uncertainty is high and wrong commitment is costly to reverse B. The decision is trivial C. Everything is known D. The vendor insists
**Q09 (O4)** A finance partner must be able to: A. Reproduce the numbers from stated assumptions B. Approve without reading C. Rely on slides only D. Skip sensitivity
**Q10 (O4)** Sensitivity analysis shows: A. Team mood B. Whether the decision changes when key assumptions change C. Security flaws D. Test coverage

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 D · Q08 A · Q09 A · Q10 B

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-008-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, defended as a business case to a role-played finance partner. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a business case whose numbers the finance partner can reproduce.
