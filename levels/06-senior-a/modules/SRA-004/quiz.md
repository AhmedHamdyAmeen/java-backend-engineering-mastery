# SRA-004 · Data ownership strategy — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 federated ownership · O2 data contracts · O3 federated governance · O4 data mesh evaluation.

**Q01 (O1)** The owner of a data set is accountable for: A. Only storage B. Its meaning, quality, access, and lifecycle C. Only backups D. Nothing once published
**Q02 (O1)** In a federated model the central data platform team: A. Owns all data sets B. Owns no tools C. Provides tools, standards, and shared infrastructure D. Approves every query
**Q03 (O1)** A copy of data held by another team should be: A. Unlabeled B. Edited freely C. Treated as the source D. Labeled as a copy and refreshed from the authoritative source
**Q04 (O2)** The most common cause of contract disputes is missing: A. Colors B. Semantics of fields C. Field names D. Team names
**Q05 (O2)** A breaking change to a data contract needs: A. Silence B. A version, notice period, and overlap C. Immediate deployment D. A new team
**Q06 (O2)** The best place to check a contract automatically is: A. The consumer after failure B. A quarterly meeting C. The producer's pipeline D. Production alerts only
**Q07 (O3)** Federated governance means: A. Central policy with local decisions by owners B. No policy C. One board decides everything D. Each team invents its own classes
**Q08 (O3)** A good measure of governance health is: A. Number of policies B. Number of meetings C. Size of the policy document D. Time to grant legitimate access and number of incidents
**Q09 (O4)** Data mesh requires: A. A single tool B. A cloud provider C. Mature domain teams and a platform with product thinking about data D. Removing all central teams
**Q10 (O4)** A sensible first step toward data mesh is: A. Rebuild every pipeline B. Fire the data team C. Buy a mesh product D. Ownership and contracts with a catalog

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 B · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 A · Q08 D · Q09 C · Q10 D

## Practical part
Six case exercises `SRA-004-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, reviewed as an ownership map and contract set. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a map where every data set has one owner and every contract includes quality SLAs.
