# STF-P2 — Influence, Communication, and Leadership

**Phase goal:** the engineer gets an organization to change without formal authority: building trust, writing proposals that get adopted, running cross-team programs, driving adoption, growing senior engineers, facilitating hard decisions, communicating with executives, leading through crises, measuring impact, and sustaining a Staff career.

**Running case:** *Platform 2.0* continues. The strategy, governance, and platform direction from STF-P1 must now be adopted by about 20 product teams who did not ask for them. Role-players act as engineering managers, skeptical tech leads, product, finance, security, and executives.

**Format:** case studies, worked sessions, and leadership role-play, with two worked sessions per module. Assessment weighs organizational leverage, not code.

**Phase total:** ~17.1 h video · ~38 h practice
