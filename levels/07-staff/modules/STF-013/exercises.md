# STF-013 · Cross-team programs — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### STF-013-LAB1 · Platform rollout program
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~4 h
1. Write the program charter for moving all teams to Platform 2.0 pipelines: outcome, scope, sponsor, success measures.
2. Define milestones with owners and done-checks; build the dependency map and mark the critical path.
3. Define the accountability routine (program review, status rules) and an escalation path.
4. The assessor injects a slip (for example, a shared service delayed by six weeks); respond.
5. Write the escalation note and the update to teams.
**Acceptance:** response protects the critical path and is communicated within one cycle.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STF-013-EX01 | 1 | Turn "migrate everyone to the new pipelines" into five milestones with owners and checks. |
| STF-013-EX02 | 1 | Decide which of three initiatives need a program and which a project, and say why. |
| STF-013-EX03 | 2 | Given eight tasks with dependencies, draw the dependency map and name the critical path. |
| STF-013-EX04 | 3 | A team lead repeatedly misses commitments. Plan how you create accountability without being their manager. |
| STF-013-EX05 | 4 | A shared service slips by six weeks. Write the escalation note with impact, options, and recommendation. |
| STF-013-EX06 | 4 | Review a Gantt chart last updated two months ago and propose a replacement that stays accurate. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** M1 pilot team in production, M2 five teams, M3 ten teams with support model proven, M4 all teams, M5 legacy retired; each with a named owner and a check such as "deploys through the new pipeline for two weeks".
- **EX02** Program: multi-team migration; project: a single team's service rewrite; judge by number of independent teams and cross-dependencies.
- **EX03** Map the chains; the longest chain by duration is critical; list slack on the others and who protects the critical chain.
- **EX04** Written commitments agreed with their manager, short regular review, early private conversation when a date is missed, and visible recognition when they deliver.
- **EX05** Impact (12 teams, M3 later by six weeks), options (lend engineers, descope, move M3), recommendation, decision needed by Friday, other party warned first.
- **EX06** Replace with a milestone board and dependency list reviewed each cycle, with RAG rules and a named owner who updates it.

Outcomes 2 and 3 are also covered by the lab slip response and accountability routine.

**Workplace evidence:** a cross-team program you led to completion.
