# STF-010 · Technical due diligence — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 rapid assessment · O2 deal-breakers versus fixable debt · O3 the report · O4 modernization plan.

**Q01 (O1)** Due diligence should focus on: A. Code style B. Variable names C. Risk to the decision D. Logo design
**Q02 (O1)** Which evidence best shows recovery capability? A. A backup restore test result B. A claim of daily backups C. A policy page D. A tool name
**Q03 (O1)** Key-person risk means: A. A person is too senior B. Critical knowledge or access sits with too few people C. A person is new D. A person is remote
**Q04 (O2)** A deal-breaker is a finding that: A. Is embarrassing B. Takes any effort C. Cannot be fixed at acceptable cost or time D. Is minor
**Q05 (O2)** Remediation estimates should include: A. A single exact number B. No numbers C. Only hours D. A range and a confidence level
**Q06 (O2)** Dependencies two years out of date are usually: A. A fixable but real risk that should be costed B. Irrelevant C. Always a deal-breaker D. A style issue
**Q07 (O3)** A report should begin with: A. Appendices B. Interview notes C. A summary and a recommendation with conditions D. A tool list
**Q08 (O3)** Why list what you could not verify? A. To fill space B. To limit liability only C. To avoid conclusions D. Readers must know the limits of the evidence
**Q09 (O4)** A modernization plan should be: A. Sized to the findings B. Identical for every target C. A full rewrite always D. Postponed
**Q10 (O3)** When challenged on confidence, you should: A. Hide uncertainty B. Insist you are certain C. State confidence, unknowns, and what access would reduce them D. End the meeting

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 A · Q03 B · Q04 C · Q05 D · Q06 A · Q07 C · Q08 D · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-010-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, with the report presented to a role-played deal team. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a report whose top risks are costed and whose recommendation states conditions.
