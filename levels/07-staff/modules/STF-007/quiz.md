# STF-007 · Technology portfolio — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 technology radar · O2 standardize, consolidate, diversify · O3 retirement · O4 innovation versus cost.

**Q01 (O1)** The four common radar rings are: A. Adopt, trial, assess, hold B. Good, better, best, done C. Java, Go, Rust, Python D. Now, soon, later, never
**Q02 (O1)** An "adopt" entry should have: A. Fashion B. Anyone's preference C. Production evidence and an owner D. A vendor logo
**Q03 (O1)** A radar reflecting personal taste is fixed by: A. Deleting it B. Publishing written criteria and adoption data C. Hiding it D. Making it mandatory
**Q04 (O2)** Standardize first where variety: A. Fits each problem B. Costs little C. Is free D. Costs a lot and gives little
**Q05 (O2)** Consolidate duplicates when: A. Savings exceed migration cost B. A leader dislikes one C. They are old D. They are new
**Q06 (O3)** A retirement should be announced with: A. A reason, a deadline, a replacement, and support B. A surprise shutdown C. Silence D. Only a date
**Q07 (O3)** Retirements without migration help usually: A. Finish early B. Cost nothing C. Slip or are ignored D. Improve morale
**Q08 (O4)** A bounded trial includes: A. No end date B. Unlimited scope C. A secret team D. Goal, time limit, success measures, and exit plan
**Q09 (O4)** Never trialing anything new leads to: A. Stagnation and skills gaps B. Lower risk always C. Faster delivery D. Lower cost always
**Q10 (O2)** Allow diversity when: A. Teams disagree B. Problems genuinely differ and fit gain exceeds support cost C. A tool is fashionable D. Budget is large
