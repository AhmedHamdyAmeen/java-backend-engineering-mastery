# STF-012 · Technical proposals — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 problem framing · O2 evidence, alternatives, costs, risks · O3 running the review · O4 writing for several audiences.

**Q01 (O1)** A proposal should open with: A. The architecture diagram B. The problem and who is affected C. The vendor comparison D. The timeline
**Q02 (O1)** The best test of your problem framing is: A. Your own confidence B. Whether two skeptical readers agree it is worth solving C. The length of the document D. Leadership approval
**Q03 (O2)** "Do nothing" should be: A. Left out B. Mentioned in a footnote C. Included as an option with its cost D. Used only when the team agrees
**Q04 (O2)** Costs and risks belong: A. In an appendix B. Visible in the main body C. Only in the executive summary D. In the review comments
**Q05 (O2)** Which statement is evidence? A. "Everyone knows pipelines are messy" B. "I prefer this tool" C. "Four audit findings last year came from pipeline drift" D. "It is industry standard"
**Q06 (O3)** Before publishing a controversial RFC you should: A. Wait for comments B. Pre-brief the key reviewers C. Ask the CTO to approve D. Shorten the review window
**Q07 (O3)** A comment you decide not to accept should get: A. No response B. A deletion C. A reasoned reply D. An escalation
**Q08 (O3)** A review that never closes: A. Is safe B. Builds consensus C. Lets teams act on rumours and the proposal dies slowly D. Is faster
**Q09 (O4)** A busy executive should find, at the top: A. The design detail B. The comment history C. The migration scripts D. A short summary with cost, benefit, and the decision needed
**Q10 (O4)** Serving several audiences is best done with: A. A layered single document B. Three separate documents C. A slide deck only D. Different versions for each team

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 B · Q07 C · Q08 C · Q09 D · Q10 A

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-012-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, assessed with the RFC quality rubric and the review outcome. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a recorded decision within the review window with major objections answered in the document.
