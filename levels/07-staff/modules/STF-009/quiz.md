# STF-009 · Engineering productivity — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 balanced measurement · O2 feedback-loop delays · O3 pipelines and tooling · O4 business case.

**Q01 (O1)** A balanced view of productivity includes: A. Lines of code B. Story points per person C. Delivery metrics, developer experience, and cognitive load D. Hours at desk
**Q02 (O1)** Individual leaderboards are avoided because they: A. Are costly B. Damage trust and invite gaming C. Are slow D. Are illegal everywhere
**Q03 (O1)** Which is a delivery metric? A. Lead time for changes B. Number of meetings C. Seat count D. Commit count per person
**Q04 (O2)** Which measurement often reveals the biggest delay? A. Typing speed B. Wait time between steps C. Test count D. Build tool version
**Q05 (O2)** Prioritize delays by: A. Loudest complaint B. Seniority C. Total hours across teams D. Alphabetical order
**Q06 (O3)** When changing a slow pipeline, you should: A. Change everything at once B. Guess C. Skip baseline D. Change one thing per run and compare to baseline
**Q07 (O3)** Why improve the shared pipeline template before one team's pipeline? A. It is easier B. A shared fix helps every team using it C. Teams prefer it D. It needs no testing
**Q08 (O3)** A good onboarding metric is: A. Number of slides B. Hours of training C. Documents written D. Time to first production change
**Q09 (O4)** A productivity business case should state: A. Assumptions and a range, with quality counter-metrics B. One optimistic number C. Only feelings D. Only cost
**Q10 (O4)** A survey without published actions: A. Builds trust B. Increases response next time C. Erodes trust and response D. Is ideal

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 A · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 D · Q07 B · Q08 D · Q09 A · Q10 C

Six case exercises `STF-009-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, reviewed as a baseline and business case. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a recorded baseline that the capstone can measure improvement against.
