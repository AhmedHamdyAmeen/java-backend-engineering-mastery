# STF-015 · Mentoring at scale — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 growing seniors through sponsorship and stretch work · O2 technical communities · O3 scalable learning materials · O4 inclusive sponsorship.

**Q01 (O1)** A sponsor differs from a mentor because a sponsor: A. Gives more advice B. Meets more often C. Reviews code D. Advocates for the person and creates opportunities
**Q02 (O1)** A good stretch assignment is: A. Far beyond current ability B. Slightly beyond current scope with support C. The task nobody wants D. Always taken back at the first problem
**Q03 (O1)** Scope created by sponsorship is best made: A. Visible, with the person's name on the outcome B. Private C. Temporary D. Anonymous
**Q04 (O2)** A community of practice needs first: A. A large kickoff B. A logo C. A tool D. A real purpose and an owner of the rhythm
**Q05 (O2)** To keep a community from depending on you: A. Run it yourself B. Rotate leadership C. Avoid decisions D. Make it optional
**Q06 (O2)** A community that no longer serves a purpose should be: A. Kept forever B. Merged silently C. Retired or reset D. Made mandatory
**Q07 (O3)** The best signal for what to document is: A. Your own preference B. Documents others have C. Questions asked repeatedly D. Executive requests
**Q08 (O3)** Which scales beyond your time? A. One-to-one coaching B. A current guide plus office hours C. Reviewing every design D. Daily check-ins
**Q09 (O4)** Why audit who you sponsor? A. To see and correct a bias toward familiar people B. To reduce workload C. For HR reports D. To avoid risk
**Q10 (O4)** Choosing people to sponsor should rest on: A. Similarity to you B. Seniority only C. Evidence of work and potential D. Who asks most

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 B · Q03 A · Q04 D · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 C · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-015-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, assessed by plan review plus mentee feedback (if mentoring a Senior learner). Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and each plan including a sponsored stretch assignment with visible scope.
