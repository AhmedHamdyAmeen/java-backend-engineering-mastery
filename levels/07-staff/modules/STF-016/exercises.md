# STF-016 · Decision facilitation — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### STF-016-LAB1 · Stuck decision
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3 h
1. The organization has argued for six months about a single observability stack; collect each role-played lead's position and underlying values.
2. Write the decision brief: options, criteria, owner, advisers, deadline.
3. Facilitate the session with the role-played leads; make trade-offs explicit and separate facts from values.
4. Check consent; if needed, use disagree and commit with recorded dissent.
5. Publish the decision record and communicate it.
**Acceptance:** decision, owner, and dissent recorded; all parties confirm commitment.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STF-016-EX01 | 1 | Diagnose why the "single CI" decision has been open for four months and write the decision brief that restarts it. |
| STF-016-EX02 | 1 | Agree criteria and weights for choosing between two logging stacks; say who decides and by when. |
| STF-016-EX03 | 2 | Two leads argue "stack A is more flexible" versus "stack B is cheaper". Separate facts from values and propose a test for each fact. |
| STF-016-EX04 | 3 | The group is split 3 to 2. Decide between more consensus-seeking and disagree and commit, and write what you say to the dissenting leads. |
| STF-016-EX05 | 4 | Write a decision record for the outcome, including dissent and a revisit trigger. |
| STF-016-EX06 | 2 | Review a meeting transcript where the facilitator argued for their option; list the process errors and corrections. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** No owner, no deadline, no criteria, cost of delay unwritten. Brief: question, options, criteria, owner, deadline, and the default if no decision by the date.
- **EX02** Criteria such as cost, query capability, operations effort, team skills; weights agreed first; owner is the platform director; deadline in two weeks.
- **EX03** Facts: price per GB, query latency, migration effort, measurable by a pilot. Values: flexibility versus cost; the owner chooses which to weigh more.
- **EX04** Check whether every voice was heard and whether the decision is reviewable; if yes, the owner decides, record dissent, review at six months with agreed metrics; thank the dissenting leads and ask for commitment.
- **EX05** Context, decision, options, reasons, owner, dissent, consequences, revisit trigger (for example cost exceeds forecast by 25%).
- **EX06** Errors: facilitator advocated, owner not named, no criteria. Corrections: restate the process, return to criteria, hand the decision to the owner.

Outcome 2 is also covered by the lab trade-off step.

**Workplace evidence:** a long-stuck decision you helped the organization make.
