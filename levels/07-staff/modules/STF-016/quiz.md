# STF-016 · Decision facilitation — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 structuring decisions · O2 explicit trade-offs · O3 consensus and disagree-and-commit · O4 recording decisions.

**Q01 (O1)** The most common reason a technical decision stays stuck is: A. Too little data B. Tooling C. No clear owner, deadline, or criteria D. Too few options
**Q02 (O1)** Criteria should be agreed: A. After comparing options B. Before comparing options C. By the facilitator alone D. After the decision
**Q03 (O1)** A decision brief names: A. Only the options B. The options and a winner C. The meeting room D. Options, criteria, owner, and deadline
**Q04 (O2)** A disagreement about measurable cost is a: A. Fact to be checked B. Value C. Personality issue D. Reason to escalate
**Q05 (O2)** A disagreement about autonomy versus consistency is mainly about: A. Facts B. Values the owner must weigh C. Tools D. Budget
**Q06 (O3)** Consent means: A. Everyone's first choice B. Majority vote C. Nobody has a serious objection D. The facilitator agrees
**Q07 (O3)** Disagree and commit requires that the dissent: A. Be hidden B. Be heard and recorded C. Be ignored D. Be resolved by vote
**Q08 (O3)** During the meeting, the facilitator should: A. Push their preferred option B. Stay silent C. Choose the option D. Manage the process and hand the decision to the owner
**Q09 (O4)** A decision record should include: A. Only the outcome B. Decision, reasons, owner, dissent, and revisit trigger C. Meeting minutes D. Attendee list only
**Q10 (O4)** A revisit trigger exists to: A. Prevent endless reopening while allowing change when facts change B. Delay implementation C. Blame the owner D. Increase meetings

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 B · Q08 D · Q09 B · Q10 A

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-016-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, assessed in a facilitation simulation. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a recorded decision with owner and dissent that all role-played leads confirm commitment to.
