# STF-001 · Staff engineering role — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 scope, horizon, leverage · O2 archetypes · O3 Staff and managers · O4 choosing work by leverage.

**Q01 (O1)** Which best describes Staff scope? A. One system you built B. A problem that spans several teams C. One team's backlog D. Only the largest service
**Q02 (O1)** Leverage is best defined as: A. Hours worked B. Lines of code C. Outcome change across many teams per unit of effort D. Seniority of the sponsor
**Q03 (O2)** A Staff engineer who extends a senior executive's technical reach is a: A. Solver B. Tech lead C. Architect D. Right hand
**Q04 (O2)** An organization on fire with repeated outages most needs which archetype first? A. Solver B. Right hand C. Tech lead of a quiet team D. Writer of long-term principles only
**Q05 (O3)** Who owns a team member's performance review? A. Staff engineer B. The manager C. The VP of Engineering only D. The team vote
**Q06 (O3)** A healthy Staff and manager boundary is: A. Staff assigns tasks B. Both own the roadmap C. Explicit agreement on who decides what D. No contact until launch
**Q07 (O4)** Which candidate usually has the highest leverage? A. A refactor in one team B. A shared fix that removes repeat incidents in twelve teams C. A demo dashboard D. A personal tool
**Q08 (O4)** Why record work you decline? A. To blame requesters B. To avoid meetings C. To make trade-offs visible D. To reduce documentation
**Q09 (O1)** Why does a Staff engineer write documents? A. They are required B. Code is unimportant C. Managers read only documents D. Writing scales thinking to many teams
**Q10 (O4)** A scoring model for candidates should include: A. Teams affected, pain, effort, and who else could solve it B. Only your interest C. Only cost D. Only the sponsor's rank

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 D · Q10 A

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-001-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, defended as a 90-day plan to a role-played VP of Engineering. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a plan whose problems name affected teams and measures.
