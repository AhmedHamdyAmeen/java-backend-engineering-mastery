# STF-001 · Staff engineering role — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### STF-001-LAB1 · First 90 days
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~2 h
1. Interview role-played managers, tech leads, and product partners using one question script.
2. Cluster the answers into problems; note which teams name each.
3. Choose the archetype the organization needs and justify it from the evidence.
4. Rank three candidate problems by leverage (teams affected, pain, effort, alternatives).
5. Write the 90-day plan and defend it to a role-played VP of Engineering.
**Acceptance:** each candidate problem names the teams affected and how impact would be measured.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STF-001-EX01 | 1 | A Senior proposes to "do Staff work" by rewriting the checkout service. Explain in half a page how scope, horizon, and leverage differ from this proposal. |
| STF-001-EX02 | 2 | Given three org profiles (new platform, firefighting, new CTO), choose the archetype for each and justify. |
| STF-001-EX03 | 3 | A manager says you are "stepping on their roadmap". Write the boundary agreement you would propose in five bullets. |
| STF-001-EX04 | 4 | Score six requests on leverage using teams affected, pain, effort, and alternatives; rank them. |
| STF-001-EX05 | 4 | Draft a reply declining a high-visibility, low-leverage request while offering an alternative. |
| STF-001-EX06 | 1, 3 | Review a colleague's 90-day plan that lists eleven projects and no managers' input; give three corrections. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Rewrite is single-team scope and short horizon, with leverage only for one team; a Staff choice would address a problem many teams share, such as how every service is built and released, and share the result.
- **EX02** New platform: architect; firefighting: solver; new CTO: right hand (with architect later). Justify with the dominant pain.
- **EX03** Managers own people and priorities; you own technical direction; share drafts before meetings; no assigning work; escalate disagreements together; review the agreement quarterly.
- **EX04** Rank by (teams affected x pain) / effort; mark items others could do as delegate; show the arithmetic.
- **EX05** Restate their goal, show the leverage comparison, offer a template or a later date, record the decision.
- **EX06** Cut to three; interview managers first; attach a measure and affected teams to each project.

Outcomes 2 and 4 are also covered by the lab plan defense; outcomes 1 and 3 by the quiz.

**Workplace evidence:** a piece of work you chose because of its leverage across teams, and the result.
