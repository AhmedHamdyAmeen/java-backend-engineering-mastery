# STF-018 · Incident and crisis leadership — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 leading multi-team incidents · O2 crisis communication · O3 strategic corrective actions · O4 improving the incident process.

**Q01 (O1)** The leader of a multi-team crisis should mainly: A. Fix the technical fault B. Coordinate, keep the whole picture, and delegate C. Write the postmortem during the incident D. Wait for executives
**Q02 (O1)** A crisis should be declared: A. Late, after the cause is known B. Never; use normal incident process C. Early, when impact and scope meet the criteria D. Only by executives
**Q03 (O1)** Executive questions during the incident should go through: A. The commander directly B. Everyone C. The workstream leads D. A liaison
**Q04 (O2)** If nothing has changed since the last update, you should: A. Send nothing B. Say nothing has changed, what is being done, and the next update time C. Wait for news D. Update the customers only
**Q05 (O2)** A customer message during an outage should: A. Speculate on the cause B. Blame a provider C. State impact, workaround, and next update in plain language D. Be delayed until resolved
**Q06 (O2)** Regulatory notification obligations should be: A. Handled after the incident B. Handled by engineers alone C. Ignored D. Checked with legal and compliance early
**Q07 (O3)** Strategic corrective actions differ from local fixes because they: A. Are cheaper B. Address causes across teams with owners and funding C. Need no tracking D. Fix only one team
**Q08 (O3)** Blameless review aims to find: A. The responsible person B. The slowest engineer C. Systemic causes D. Legal liability
**Q09 (O4)** Which measure shows incident process improvement? A. Number of meetings B. Number of incidents only C. Team size D. Time to declare and time to first update
**Q10 (O4)** Looking after responders in a long incident includes: A. Rest rotation and credit for the work B. Blame C. Single hero responder D. Staying until done

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 D · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 D · Q10 A

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-018-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, assessed in a crisis simulation. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a corrective-action plan whose actions address systemic causes and have owners and funding.
