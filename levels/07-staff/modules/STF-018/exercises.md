# STF-018 · Incident and crisis leadership — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### STF-018-LAB1 · Crisis game day
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3.5 h
1. Prepare the crisis roles, channels, update cadence, and runbook.
2. Lead a multi-team, multi-hour simulated crisis with role-played executives and a partner (the assessor injects new facts).
3. Communicate with executives, customers (draft messages), and a regulator contact if relevant.
4. Run a blameless review and write the strategic corrective-action plan with owners and funding.
5. Write three changes to the incident process itself.
**Acceptance:** actions address systemic causes and have owners and funding.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STF-018-EX01 | 1 | Decide whether three scenarios are incidents or crises, and write the roles you activate for each. |
| STF-018-EX02 | 1 | During a payment outage, you find yourself debugging. Write what you do to return to leadership. |
| STF-018-EX03 | 2 | Draft the first executive update and customer message for a two-hour provider outage. |
| STF-018-EX04 | 2 | A regulator requires notification within a deadline. Plan who is involved and when. |
| STF-018-EX05 | 3 | Convert three local fixes from a postmortem into systemic actions with owners and funding. |
| STF-018-EX06 | 4 | Review the crisis process after a game day and write three improvements with measures. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Crisis: many teams, customer or revenue impact, external parties. Roles: commander, communications lead, liaison, scribe, workstream leads.
- **EX02** Hand the technical thread to the workstream lead, restore the shared timeline, set the next sync, and keep to coordination.
- **EX03** Executive: impact, what we know, actions, next update in 30 minutes. Customer: plain language, what is affected, workaround, next update; no speculation.
- **EX04** Involve legal and compliance at declaration; check obligations and deadlines for the jurisdiction (verify); designate the owner; record the timeline for the notice.
- **EX05** Example: "add timeout to service X" becomes "shared client library with timeouts and circuit breakers adopted by all teams"; owner, funding, due date, check via game day.
- **EX06** Improvements such as faster declaration criteria, a standing executive liaison, updated contact lists; measures: time to declare, time to first update, action completion.

Outcome 4 is also covered by the lab process changes; responder wellbeing is covered in the quiz.

**Workplace evidence:** a major incident you led and the organizational change that followed.
