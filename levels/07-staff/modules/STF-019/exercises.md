# STF-019 · Measuring impact — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### STF-019-LAB1 · Success metrics
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3.5 h
1. Take the STF-009 baseline and choose metrics for adoption, lead time, incidents, cost, and developer satisfaction.
2. For each, write the definition, data source, owner, target, and whether it is leading or lagging.
3. Build a one-page dashboard design with trend, target, definitions, and freshness.
4. Write the attribution method (control group or prior trend) and a mock impact report with honest caveats.
5. Review with the assessor.
**Acceptance:** becomes the capstone's success metrics; each metric has a data source.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STF-019-EX01 | 1 | Choose five success measures for the golden path with definitions and baselines, and drop two vanity metrics from a proposed list. |
| STF-019-EX02 | 1 | A sponsor wants one number. Propose it, with its limits, and say what you will also show. |
| STF-019-EX03 | 2 | Pair three leading indicators with the lagging indicators they should predict, and say how you will test the link. |
| STF-019-EX04 | 2 | Incidents were already falling 5% a quarter before the program; they fell 12% after. State how much you claim and how you say it. |
| STF-019-EX05 | 3 | Design a one-page dashboard: metrics, layout, data sources, and what you leave out. |
| STF-019-EX06 | 4 | Write the "what did not work" section of an impact report where satisfaction stayed flat and cost rose. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Measures: teams on golden path, lead time for changes, incident count and duration, cost per service, developer satisfaction. Drop: lines of platform code, number of meetings.
- **EX02** Single number: lead time to production, with the limits (varies by team, does not show reliability); also show incidents and adoption.
- **EX03** Examples: golden-path adoption predicts lead time; support questions predict satisfaction; test by comparing adopters and non-adopters over time.
- **EX04** Claim about 7 percentage points beyond the trend, with confidence "plausible, not proven" and a note on other changes.
- **EX05** Five tiles with baseline, target, trend, definition, last updated; data from CI, incident tool, billing, and surveys; leave out activity counts.
- **EX06** State facts, probable reasons (migration burden, support gaps), costs, and the planned changes with owners; do not hide or soften.

Outcomes 2 and 3 are also covered by the lab; outcome 4 by lab mock report.

**Workplace evidence:** an impact report you produced for work you led.
