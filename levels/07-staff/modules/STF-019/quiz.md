# STF-019 · Measuring impact — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 success measures up front · O2 leading and lagging indicators, attribution · O3 impact dashboards · O4 honest reporting.

**Q01 (O1)** Success measures should be defined: A. After the results B. Before starting the work, with a baseline C. By the sponsor only D. At the final review
**Q02 (O1)** Which is a vanity metric? A. Lead time for changes B. Incident count C. Number of platform meetings held D. Cost per service
**Q03 (O1)** Each measure needs: A. A color B. A target only C. A tool name D. A definition, data source, and owner
**Q04 (O2)** A leading indicator is: A. An early signal that predicts results B. A late confirmation C. A financial statement D. A survey
**Q05 (O2)** For steering a quarter you rely mostly on: A. Leading indicators B. Lagging indicators C. Annual reports D. Opinions
**Q06 (O2)** To estimate your own contribution, you can: A. Assume all change is yours B. Compare with a control group or the prior trend C. Ask the sponsor D. Use the largest number
**Q07 (O3)** A trusted dashboard shows: A. Only good news B. Many metrics C. Few metrics with definitions, baseline, target, and freshness D. Manual claims
**Q08 (O3)** Dashboard data should come from: A. Manual claims B. Systems of record C. Team estimates D. Memory
**Q09 (O4)** A trend that began before your work: A. Is yours B. Is irrelevant C. Should be ignored D. Should be shown, with only the increment claimed
**Q10 (O4)** An honest impact report: A. Includes what did not work and why B. Reports only wins C. Hides uncertainty D. Uses one metric

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 A · Q06 B · Q07 C · Q08 B · Q09 D · Q10 A

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-019-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, assessed in a metrics review. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a metrics set where every metric has a data source, a baseline from STF-009, and a target.
