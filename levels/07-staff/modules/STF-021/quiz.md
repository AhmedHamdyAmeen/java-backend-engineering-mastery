# STF-021 · AI governance and engineering productivity — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 AI governance model · O2 evaluation and quality standards · O3 guardrails in the platform · O4 measuring AI's effect.

**Q01 (O1)** Banning AI tools outright often: A. Removes all risk B. Reduces cost C. Pushes use out of sight where data handling is unmanaged D. Improves quality
**Q02 (O1)** Data rules should: A. Classify data and say which tools each class may use B. Be unwritten C. Apply to only one team D. Replace approvals
**Q03 (O1)** Who decides how a team uses an approved tool? A. A central board for every prompt B. The team, within the data rules C. Legal alone D. The vendor
**Q04 (O2)** AI-generated code in a pull request: A. Skips review B. Is the tool's responsibility C. Gets the same review, tests, and scans; the author is accountable D. Is rejected
**Q05 (O2)** An AI feature needs: A. An evaluation set, thresholds, and monitoring B. No testing C. Only a demo D. Only a vendor SLA
**Q06 (O3)** An AI gateway helps by: A. Slowing teams B. Replacing engineers C. Hiding costs D. Enforcing policy, redacting data, and logging use in one place
**Q07 (O3)** A rule with no enforcement point or owner is: A. Strong B. A wish; it will not be followed reliably C. Automatic D. Legal advice
**Q08 (O3)** Governance that reviews every prompt in a central board: A. Scales well B. Is ideal C. Becomes a bottleneck D. Is required
**Q09 (O4)** Before rollout you should capture: A. Nothing B. Baselines for delivery, quality, and experience C. Only vendor claims D. Only cost
**Q10 (O4)** Which pairing is honest reporting? A. One team's anecdote as the headline B. A single percentage with no caveats C. Gains only D. Gains alongside quality counter-metrics and caveats

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 A · Q03 B · Q04 C · Q05 A · Q06 D · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 B · Q10 D

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-021-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, reviewed as a governance proposal. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a proposal in which every rule has an enforcement point or owner and the measurement plan has baselines and quality counter-metrics.
