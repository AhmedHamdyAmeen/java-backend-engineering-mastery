# STF-022 · Leading an AI adoption program — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 program design · O2 fears and resistance · O3 adoption risk · O4 reporting and adjusting.

**Q01 (O1)** Leadership sets "80% of engineers using the AI tool". A better target is: A. 90% usage B. Outcome measures such as lead time and defect rate against a baseline C. More licenses D. Number of training sessions
**Q02 (O1)** Champions are most effective when they: A. Volunteer without time allocation B. Are chosen by management only C. Are respected engineers with protected time D. Work alone
**Q03 (O1)** Enablement works best when it is: A. A one-time video B. Hands-on, on real code, with clear data rules C. A policy document D. Optional and unannounced
**Q04 (O2)** An engineer worries AI tools will deskill juniors. The best response is: A. "This is resistance" B. Promise there is no risk C. Ignore it D. Acknowledge, share evidence and uncertainty, and add learning safeguards
**Q05 (O2)** Opt-in pilots help trust because they: A. Reduce cost B. Replace policy C. Give people choice and real evidence D. Avoid reporting
**Q06 (O3)** Which should be decided before the rollout? A. Pause triggers and the incident plan B. The logo C. The final headcount D. The vendor renewal
**Q07 (O3)** A secret was pasted into an AI tool. The first action is: A. Write a report B. Rotate the secret and assess exposure C. Wait for the monthly review D. Ask the engineer to delete the chat
**Q08 (O3)** Code review for AI-assisted code should: A. Be skipped B. Be automated only C. Remain mandatory D. Be optional for seniors
**Q09 (O4)** An honest executive report: A. States outcomes, trade-offs, and unknowns B. Shows only gains C. Reports usage only D. Hides incidents
**Q10 (O4)** Defects rise in pilot teams and the pause trigger is met. You should: A. Wait a month B. Blame the teams C. Expand to dilute the effect D. Pause expansion, tell sponsors, and investigate

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 B · Q04 D · Q05 C · Q06 A · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 A · Q10 D

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-022-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab with an assessor-injected event, assessed by program review plus an incident-response simulation. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a response that follows the risk plan with an executive report stating outcomes and trade-offs.
