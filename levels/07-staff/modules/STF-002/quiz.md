# STF-002 · Technical strategy — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 business priorities to investment · O2 evidence-based strategy · O3 explicit trade-offs · O4 endorsement and refresh.

**Q01 (O1)** The four common business priorities used to anchor engineering bets are: A. Growth, margin, risk, speed B. Fun, tools, headcount, size C. Java, cloud, data, UI D. Cost, color, culture, calendar
**Q02 (O1)** A good engineering bet states: A. Only the technology B. A headcount ask only C. The priority it serves and the measure it moves D. A vendor name
**Q03 (O2)** A strategy has these three parts: A. Roadmap, budget, hiring B. Principles, tools, schedule C. Goals, owners, dates D. Diagnosis, guiding policies, coherent actions
**Q04 (O2)** The best evidence base mixes: A. Numbers and interviews from many teams B. One loud team's opinion C. Vendor brochures D. Last quarter's outage only
**Q05 (O2)** Teams define "incident" differently. First step: A. Rank them anyway B. Agree one definition and re-extract data C. Ignore incidents D. Drop the data
**Q06 (O3)** What most proves a strategy is real? A. A long list of projects B. A launch date C. A named thing the organization stops doing D. A new logo
**Q07 (O3)** Why make the stop list public? A. Hidden stops cause surprise and resistance B. It embarrasses teams C. It is legally required D. It reduces data needs
**Q08 (O4)** Pre-wiring means: A. Wiring the network early B. Public debate before private talks C. Talking to leaders one to one before the group meeting D. Skipping approval
**Q09 (O4)** A strategy should be refreshed: A. Never B. Only after failure C. Every week D. On a regular cycle against new evidence
**Q10 (O1)** Which is a weak sign in a strategy? A. Two or three funded bets B. Each bet traces to a goal C. Ten equal priorities D. A measure per bet

## Answers
Q01 A · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 A · Q08 C · Q09 D · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-002-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, defended as a strategy to a role-played CTO and CPO. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a strategy in which every action traces to evidence and a priority, with at least one explicit stop.
