# STF-004 · Platform strategy — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 scope from journeys and toil · O2 paved roads and escape hatches · O3 platform as a product · O4 sizing, staffing, and funding.

**Q01 (O1)** Platform scope should be driven mainly by: A. Technology fashion B. Platform team interests C. Executive preference D. Developer journey and toil data
**Q02 (O1)** Toil is best described as: A. Repetitive manual work without lasting value B. Hard design work C. Code review D. Meetings
**Q03 (O1)** Pain should be ranked by: A. The loudest complaint B. Total hours lost across teams C. Seniority of the complainer D. Age of the issue
**Q04 (O2)** A paved road wins mainly because it is: A. Mandatory B. Cheaper to audit C. Easier and safer than the alternative D. Newer
**Q05 (O2)** Escape hatches exist so that: A. Teams with real needs can leave the path, taking on more responsibility B. Nobody uses the platform C. Security is skipped D. The platform is optional for all
**Q06 (O3)** Which is a platform product measure? A. Number of meetings B. Adoption and developer time saved C. Lines of platform code D. Number of tickets closed alone
**Q07 (O3)** A platform without product management most likely: A. Ships faster B. Needs no support C. Builds what engineers find interesting D. Is always adopted
**Q08 (O3)** Build, assemble, or buy: build only when: A. The tool is fun B. The need is specific and no good option exists C. A vendor is expensive D. A team has spare time
**Q09 (O4)** A platform business case compares: A. Team cost with hours saved and incidents avoided B. Server count with team count C. Team size with seniority D. Brand with price
**Q10 (O4)** Platform support load: A. Falls as adoption rises B. Is zero for paved roads C. Is the product team's job D. Grows with adoption and must be planned

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 A · Q03 B · Q04 C · Q05 A · Q06 B · Q07 C · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 D

Six case exercises `STF-004-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, defended as a platform strategy to role-played leadership. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a strategy in which each capability is backed by data from at least three teams.
