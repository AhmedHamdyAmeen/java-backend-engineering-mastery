# STF-011 · Influence without authority — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 trust and credibility · O2 stakeholder mapping · O3 negotiation · O4 conflict management.

**Q01 (O1)** The best way to build credibility with a team before asking them to change is to: A. Quote leadership support B. Send a long announcement C. Solve a real problem of theirs first D. Wait to be invited
**Q02 (O1)** Which breaks trust fastest? A. Saying "I don't know" B. Missing a date you promised without warning C. Asking questions D. Sharing credit
**Q03 (O1)** Borrowing a VP's authority to get adoption mainly leads to: A. Lasting commitment B. Hidden objections and reluctant compliance C. Faster learning D. Lower cost
**Q04 (O2)** A stakeholder with high influence and low interest should be: A. Ignored B. Kept informed early to avoid surprises C. Asked to do the work D. Treated as a blocker
**Q05 (O2)** A stakeholder map should be: A. Written once B. Shared publicly with ratings C. Updated after each conversation D. Replaced by an org chart
**Q06 (O3)** In negotiation, an "interest" is: A. The stated demand B. The need behind the demand C. A deadline D. A threat
**Q07 (O3)** A good negotiation ends with: A. A handshake B. A written commitment: who, what, when C. A promise to meet again D. An escalation
**Q08 (O4)** Disagreeing with a leader is best done: A. In the all-hands B. By ignoring the decision C. In private, with evidence and an option D. By waiting silently
**Q09 (O4)** A skeptical tech lead's objections are best treated as: A. Obstruction B. Free review that improves the design C. A reason to avoid them D. A personal attack
**Q10 (O4)** Persuading mainly by sending more messages and calling more meetings is: A. Effective B. Required C. A sign the case is weak and a risk to trust D. Good practice

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 B · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 B · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 B · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-011-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, scored by the role-played skeptics and an observer. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and all three conversations ending with a commitment or recorded objection.
