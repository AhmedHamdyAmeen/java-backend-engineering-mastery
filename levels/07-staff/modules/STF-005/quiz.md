# STF-005 · Organization-wide reliability — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 systemic risks from incidents · O2 reliability program · O3 operational maturity · O4 reporting reliability as business risk.

**Q01 (O1)** Eleven incidents in different teams share "no safe rollback". This is: A. A one-off B. Bad luck C. Individual error D. A systemic cause
**Q02 (O1)** "Human error" as a root cause is weak because it: A. Is too long B. Does not ask what made the error easy and undetected C. Blames the tool D. Is a legal term
**Q03 (O1)** Reliability measured only as uptime: A. Is enough B. Always rises C. Hides degraded user experience D. Is the best SLO
**Q04 (O2)** Which is a measurable program target? A. Improve postmortems B. Be more reliable C. Try harder D. 90% of postmortem actions closed within 60 days
**Q05 (O2)** A reliability program should provide: A. Policy only B. Templates, tooling, and coaching as well as policy C. Fines D. Weekly all-hands
**Q06 (O3)** A maturity model is used to: A. Direct help where it matters B. Rank and punish teams C. Replace SLOs D. Audit individuals
**Q07 (O3)** Teams to prioritize for help are those: A. With the fewest engineers B. With the newest tools C. Owning critical customer paths with low maturity D. Who ask least
**Q08 (O4)** Leadership reporting should translate reliability into: A. Business risk with trends B. Raw alert counts C. Server logs D. Names of on-call people
**Q09 (O4)** An effective quarterly review presents: A. Twenty metrics B. No decisions C. Only good news D. A few metrics tied to risk and specific decisions needed
**Q10 (O2)** On-call health includes measuring: A. Number of dashboards B. Pages per shift, out-of-hours pages, and noisy alert share C. Git commits D. Salary

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 D · Q05 B · Q06 A · Q07 C · Q08 A · Q09 D · Q10 B

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-005-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, reviewed as a reliability program. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a program whose targets are measurable and tied to the three systemic causes.
