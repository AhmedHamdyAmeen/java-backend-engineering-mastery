# STF-014 · Organizational change — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 adoption planning · O2 incentives · O3 resistance as information · O4 feedback loops.

**Q01 (O1)** The outcome of a platform rollout is best measured by: A. Features shipped B. Documents published C. Training sessions held D. Teams actually using it and the results they get
**Q02 (O1)** Good pilot teams are: A. The most friendly only B. Willing, representative, and visible C. The biggest D. Chosen by lottery
**Q03 (O1)** Proof points from pilots are used to: A. Persuade the practical majority B. Report to the board only C. Replace documentation D. Justify a mandate
**Q04 (O2)** A team is rewarded only for feature delivery. The best first move is to: A. Mandate migration B. Ignore them C. Escalate to their VP D. Lower the migration cost and show the speed benefit
**Q05 (O2)** A mandate is best when: A. Used first B. Used after tooling exists, with a date and retirement of the old system C. Sent by email D. Never possible
**Q06 (O3)** Resistance should be treated as: A. Obstruction B. Noise C. Information about unaddressed causes D. A personality issue
**Q07 (O3)** "We lose control over our pipeline" is mainly resistance from: A. Autonomy concerns B. Cost C. Unclear benefit D. Past failure
**Q08 (O3)** Silence from a team most likely means: A. Agreement B. Success C. Nothing important D. Possible quiet non-adoption to investigate
**Q09 (O4)** Closing the feedback loop means: A. Collecting more surveys B. Telling people what you changed because of their feedback C. Ending the program D. Reporting only to sponsors
**Q10 (O4)** Stop criteria should be agreed: A. When the program fails B. After the budget is spent C. At the start, with the sponsor D. By the platform team alone

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 B · Q03 A · Q04 D · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 A · Q08 D · Q09 B · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-014-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, assessed in a roadmap review. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a roadmap where each resistance cause has a response and adoption has metrics.
