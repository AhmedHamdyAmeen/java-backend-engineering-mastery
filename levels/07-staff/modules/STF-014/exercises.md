# STF-014 · Organizational change — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### STF-014-LAB1 · Adoption roadmap
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3.5 h
1. Define the pilot selection criteria and choose pilot teams from the 20-team list with reasons.
2. Write the adoption waves, proof points, and the incentive plan per stakeholder group.
3. Build a resistance register with a probable cause and a response for each.
4. Define adoption metrics, the feedback rhythm, and stop criteria.
5. Present the roadmap for review; revise.
**Acceptance:** becomes the capstone's adoption roadmap; each resistance cause has a response.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STF-014-EX01 | 1 | Three months after announcement only 2 of 20 teams use the golden path. Diagnose the plan failure and propose a pilot-based restart. |
| STF-014-EX02 | 1 | Choose three pilot teams from a table of six (stack, manager support, risk, visibility) and justify the choice. |
| STF-014-EX03 | 2 | A team is rewarded only for feature delivery. List incentives to add or costs to remove so migration fits their goals. |
| STF-014-EX04 | 3 | Classify five resistance statements by cause (autonomy, cost, benefit, trust, fear) and write a response to each. |
| STF-014-EX05 | 4 | Design a monthly adoption review one-pager and list the metrics, including a sentiment signal. |
| STF-014-EX06 | 4 | Adoption plateaus at 9 of 20 teams. Decide whether to push, change, or pause, and write the stop criteria you should have agreed at the start. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Announcement without support, no proof points, no migration tools, no incentives. Restart: three pilots, strong support, publish results, then expand.
- **EX02** Pick one easy-and-willing team, one representative mid-complexity, one harder stack with a supportive manager; avoid all friendly or all risky. Reasoning recorded.
- **EX03** Remove cost with migration tooling and pair help; add benefit by showing release speed; raise priority through the manager with a date; no mandate before tooling exists.
- **EX04** Examples: "we lose control" (autonomy: add an escape hatch); "too much work" (cost: tooling); "no benefit" (show data); "last platform failed" (trust: small wins, SLOs); "blame if it breaks" (fear: shared ownership).
- **EX05** Metrics: teams migrated, services on path, lead time, incident count, support requests, survey score; top friction points and changes made.
- **EX06** Diagnose friction first; change tooling or scope; pause the mandate; criteria such as "fewer than 12 teams by month 6 triggers scope review with the sponsor".

Outcome 3 is also covered by the lab register.

**Workplace evidence:** an adoption you drove, with adoption numbers over time.
