# STF-020 · Staff career development — Lecture scripts

**Video:** 96 min · **Running case:** *Platform 2.0* — the strategy, governance, and platform direction from STF-P1 must now be adopted by about 20 product teams who did not ask for them; role-players act as engineering managers, skeptical tech leads, product, finance, security, and executives.
Format: **Goal** · **Key points** · **Show** · **Check**.

---

## STF-020-L01 · How Staff promotions are decided (12 min)
**Goal:** understand what promotion committees look for.

**Key points**
- Committees look for sustained scope, impact, and influence beyond the team, not activity.
- Evidence must be visible to people who do not know you: documents, metrics, peer feedback.
- Sponsors speak for you in calibration; they need material.
- Criteria and process differ by company; ask for the written ladder.

**Show:** a table of criteria (scope, impact, influence, technical depth, leadership) with examples of strong and weak evidence for each.

**Check:** Why does evidence have to be visible to others? *(Decision makers often do not know your work and rely on artifacts.)*

---

## STF-020-L02 · Building a promotion packet (12 min)
**Goal:** assemble a packet that shows outcomes.

**Key points**
- Structure: summary of scope, three to five major outcomes, evidence, peer voices.
- Each claim has an artifact or number: RFC, dashboard, adoption chart.
- Write outcomes ("20 teams reduced lead time by X"), not activities ("held meetings").
- Include honest context: what was your part, what was the team's.

**Show:** a packet outline with one claim written as activity and rewritten as outcome with evidence.

**Check:** How do you turn an activity into an outcome? *(State the change it caused and prove it with a number or artifact.)*

---

## STF-020-L03 · Influence mapping (10 min)
**Goal:** see your influence network and close gaps.

**Key points**
- Map whom you influence, who influences you, and who knows your work.
- Find gaps: teams, leaders, or functions with no relationship.
- Plan small actions: collaborations, talks, reviews.
- Revisit every six months.

**Show:** a network diagram of people and teams with relationship strength, and three gap-closing actions.

**Check:** What does an influence map reveal? *(Where your reach is strong and where it has gaps.)*

---

## STF-020-L04 · Staff, Principal, manager, or architect (12 min)
**Goal:** choose a path deliberately.

**Key points**
- Staff: technical leadership across teams; Principal: company-level scope; manager: grows people and runs teams; architect: designs solutions and governs standards (see ARC-001).
- Choose by the work you want to do daily, not by title.
- Try the work: lead a program, manage temporarily, or do architecture reviews.
- Switching is possible; paths overlap.

**Show:** a comparison table of daily work, success measures, and typical frustrations for each path.

**Check:** What should drive the choice? *(The kind of work you want to do and are good at.)*

---

## STF-020-L05 · Sustainable leadership (10 min)
**Goal:** sustain the role without burnout.

**Key points**
- The job pulls you into meetings and fires; protect focus time.
- Say no, delegate, and choose where to be present.
- Rest and boundaries; watch for signs of burnout.
- Build a support network of peers.

**Show:** a weekly calendar with protected deep-work blocks, meeting limits, and recovery time.

**Check:** Why protect focus time? *(Deep work produces the designs and writing that create your leverage.)*

---

## STF-020-L06 · Staying technical (10 min)
**Goal:** keep technical depth while leading.

**Key points**
- Choose a small area to stay hands-on: a prototype, a review, an on-call shadow.
- Read code and incident reviews; do not rely on slides.
- Learn new areas deliberately (for example AI-assisted engineering).
- Do technical work that serves the organization, not hobby work.

**Show:** a quarterly learning and hands-on plan with one prototype, two deep reviews, and one new topic.

**Check:** Why stay hands-on? *(Credibility and judgment depend on current technical understanding.)*

---

## STF-020-L07 · Demo: reviewing a Staff promotion packet with a calibration panel (15 min)
**Goal:** review a packet and defend it; prepares STF-020-LAB1.

**Steps**
1. Read the packet against the criteria: scope, impact, influence, depth.
2. Mark each claim: evidenced, weakly evidenced, or unsupported.
3. Ask what a skeptical panel member would question.
4. **Planned mistake:** a packet listing talks given and RFCs written without results. Fix: rewrite as outcomes with metrics.
5. Rehearse the presentation with answers to the panel's questions.
6. Finalize with peer feedback and a sponsor.

---

## STF-020-L08 · Demo: planning a quarter that protects deep work (15 min)
**Goal:** plan time to keep focus and balance; prepares STF-020-LAB1.

**Steps**
1. List commitments: reviews, program meetings, mentoring, incidents.
2. Rank by leverage and drop or delegate the lowest.
3. Block deep-work time for two outcomes (RFC and metrics).
4. **Planned mistake:** a calendar with every request accepted. Fix: negotiate a limit and say no to three items with reasons.
5. Add recovery and learning time.
6. Review monthly and adjust.
