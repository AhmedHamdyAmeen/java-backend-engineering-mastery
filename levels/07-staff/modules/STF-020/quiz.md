# STF-020 · Staff career development — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 promotion evidence · O2 influence network · O3 choosing a path · O4 sustaining the role.

**Q01 (O1)** Promotion committees mainly look for: A. Hours worked B. Number of meetings C. Sustained scope, impact, and influence with evidence D. Seniority
**Q02 (O1)** "Gave five talks" is: A. An outcome B. An activity that needs a result to count C. Enough D. Irrelevant
**Q03 (O1)** Every claim in a packet should have: A. A superlative B. A date only C. A peer quote only D. An artifact or metric
**Q04 (O2)** An influence map helps you: A. Rank colleagues B. Avoid meetings C. Find gaps in relationships and plan actions D. Replace your manager
**Q05 (O2)** A gap in your influence network is best closed by: A. Waiting B. A small collaboration, review, or talk C. Sending an email to the VP D. Ignoring it
**Q06 (O3)** The architect path mainly involves: A. Managing people B. Company-level scope C. Designing solutions and governing standards D. Operating production
**Q07 (O3)** The best basis for choosing a path is: A. The title B. Pay only C. Peer opinion D. The daily work you want to do and are good at
**Q08 (O3)** A good way to test whether management suits you is: A. Guess B. Lead a team or program temporarily C. Read about it D. Ask HR
**Q09 (O4)** To sustain a Staff role you should: A. Accept every request B. Protect focus time and set limits C. Avoid technical work D. Work longer hours
**Q10 (O4)** Staying technical while leading is best done by: A. Reading slides B. Delegating all technical work C. Choosing a small hands-on area and reviewing real code D. Only managing

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 D · Q04 C · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 D · Q08 B · Q09 B · Q10 C

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-020-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, assessed by a calibration panel. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a packet in which every claim is backed by an artifact or metric.
