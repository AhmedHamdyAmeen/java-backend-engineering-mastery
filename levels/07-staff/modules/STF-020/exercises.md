# STF-020 · Staff career development — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### STF-020-LAB1 · Promotion packet
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3 h
1. Choose three to five outcomes from the capstone and your real work.
2. Write the packet: scope summary, outcomes, evidence (documents, metrics, adoption data), peer voices.
3. Add your influence map, gap actions, and a short note on your path choice.
4. Add a sustainability plan: focus time and technical depth.
5. Present to a role-played calibration panel and answer challenges.
**Acceptance:** every claim backed by an artifact or metric.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STF-020-EX01 | 1 | Rewrite five activity statements as outcome statements with evidence. |
| STF-020-EX02 | 1 | Review a sample packet and mark each claim evidenced, weak, or unsupported. |
| STF-020-EX03 | 2 | Draw your influence map and write three actions to close gaps. |
| STF-020-EX04 | 3 | Compare Staff, Principal, manager, and architect paths for your preferences; decide and list one experiment to test it. |
| STF-020-EX05 | 4 | Plan a quarter with protected deep-work blocks and three things you will decline. |
| STF-020-EX06 | 4 | Write a quarterly plan to stay technical: one prototype, two reviews, one new topic. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** "Ran the architecture forum" becomes "Forum decisions cut review time from 3 weeks to 1 and removed duplicate tools in 6 teams" with a link to the decision records.
- **EX02** Evidenced: linked RFC and metric. Weak: "teams liked it" without survey. Unsupported: claims with no artifact; ask for evidence or remove.
- **EX03** Gaps may include security, finance, or a region; actions: a joint review, a talk, a shared working group.
- **EX04** Answer by daily work: for example Staff if you enjoy cross-team design; manager if you enjoy growing people; test by leading a team for a quarter or doing architecture reviews.
- **EX05** Two blocks of four hours for deep work, meeting limit, decline three requests with reasons and alternatives, schedule recovery.
- **EX06** Prototype of golden-path feature, two incident or design reviews done in depth, one learning topic with a short write-up.

Outcome 2 is also covered by the lab influence map.

**Workplace evidence:** a promotion packet or performance review at Staff scope.
