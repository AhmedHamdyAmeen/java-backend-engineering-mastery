# STF-017 · Executive communication — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### STF-017-LAB1 · Platform 2.0 business case
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3.5 h
1. Build the cost model: costs, benefits, assumptions, three scenarios, payback, using the STF-009 baseline.
2. Write the cost and risk narrative and the comparison with alternatives and doing nothing.
3. Write the story and a 15-minute presentation with backup slides.
4. Present to role-played CEO, CFO, and CTO; answer questions.
5. Agree the reporting and relationship plan (quarterly update).
**Acceptance:** funding decision reached; cost model reproducible by finance.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STF-017-EX01 | 1 | Given a cost table, build a business case with low, expected, and high scenarios and a payback period. |
| STF-017-EX02 | 1 | List five benefits of Platform 2.0, convert each to money or a risk measure, and state the assumption and how it will be checked. |
| STF-017-EX03 | 2 | Rewrite a technology-first pitch ("we need Kubernetes operators") as a three-minute business story. |
| STF-017-EX04 | 3 | Prepare a comparison of three options (do nothing, extend current, Platform 2.0) that the CFO can set beside other investments. |
| STF-017-EX05 | 3 | Write a one-slide risk summary in business terms for the platform program. |
| STF-017-EX06 | 4 | The year's spend is 20% above plan. Write the update to the CFO and the plan for ongoing reporting. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Show inputs, assumptions, three scenarios, and payback; all computations in a sheet finance can reproduce; note the main assumption that drives the result.
- **EX02** Examples: lead time per service (engineer-days saved at loaded cost), incident hours avoided (loss per hour), audit findings avoided, onboarding time, cloud savings; each with an owner and a measurement date.
- **EX03** Open with the business effect (launches wait weeks), the cost, the decision, the result expected, and the next step; keep Kubernetes in backup.
- **EX04** Table with cost per year, risk reduction, time to value, and what must be true; include the risks of the investment.
- **EX05** Likelihood and impact in outage hours, audit findings, and delivery delay; mitigations and owners.
- **EX06** State the overspend, the cause, what you changed, the revised forecast, and what benefits are on track; schedule a quarterly review.

Outcome 4 is also covered by the lab relationship plan and the quiz.

**Workplace evidence:** an engineering investment you secured with a business case.
