# STF-017 · Executive communication — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 engineering business case · O2 technical storytelling · O3 cost and risk narratives · O4 ongoing executive relationships.

**Q01 (O1)** A business case should show costs, benefits, and risks, and also: A. The code B. The tool names C. How benefits will be checked later D. The team photo
**Q02 (O1)** Presenting a cost range (low, expected, high) is: A. Weak B. Confusing C. Unnecessary D. More honest and more credible
**Q03 (O1)** An executive-level presentation should open with: A. The decision requested and why it matters B. The architecture C. The history D. The team
**Q04 (O2)** A good technical story starts with: A. The technology B. The business situation or problem C. The vendor D. The roadmap
**Q05 (O2)** Which is better evidence in a story? A. "Many teams are slow" B. "The platform is modern" C. "Everyone agrees" D. "A payments feature waited three weeks for pipeline setup"
**Q06 (O3)** Risk for executives should be expressed: A. In technical jargon B. In business terms such as outage hours and audit findings C. As a color D. As a long list
**Q07 (O3)** A comparison for finance should include: A. Only your option B. Only costs C. Do nothing and alternatives on the same criteria D. Only benefits
**Q08 (O3)** The CFO can't reproduce your cost model. This is: A. Acceptable B. Normal C. A reason to hide it D. A serious flaw to fix
**Q09 (O4)** Bad news to executives should be reported: A. Early, with options B. At year end C. Never D. Only if asked
**Q10 (O4)** Maintaining an executive relationship means: A. Meeting only to ask for money B. Regular short updates and following up on promised benefits C. Avoiding them D. Sending long reports

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 D · Q03 A · Q04 B · Q05 D · Q06 B · Q07 C · Q08 D · Q09 A · Q10 B

## Practical part
Six case exercises `STF-017-EX01` to `EX06` plus the lab, assessed by a presentation scored on the COM and LEA dimensions. Pass: at least five of six exercises correct, quiz at least 8/10, and a funding decision reached with a cost model finance can reproduce.
