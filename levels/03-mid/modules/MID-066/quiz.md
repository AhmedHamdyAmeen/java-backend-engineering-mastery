# MID-066 · Technical collaboration — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 reviewing code · O2 feedback and design reviews · O3 mentoring · O4 disagree and commit.

**Q01 (O1)** Human reviewers should mainly focus on: A. Formatting B. Correctness, design, and operability C. Line count D. Commit message grammar
**Q02 (O1)** A mistake in reviewing: A. Reading the tests B. Asking questions C. Approving without reading the tests D. Labeling nits
**Q03 (O1)** A comment that rewrites the author's whole method is: A. Ideal B. Required C. Faster for everyone D. Usually unhelpful; describe the problem and suggest a direction
**Q04 (O2)** The label `nit:` means: A. Blocking B. Minor and optional C. Security risk D. Question
**Q05 (O2)** Good feedback is: A. About the person B. General C. Specific and about the work D. Public only
**Q06 (O2)** In a design review the author should begin with: A. The code B. Details of syntax C. A demo only D. The problem, constraints, and the feedback wanted
**Q07 (O3)** When pairing with a junior: A. You drive all the time B. They usually drive while you ask questions C. You stay silent D. You skip explanation
**Q08 (O3)** A well-scoped task for a junior has: A. A clear outcome and a little stretch B. No acceptance criteria C. A large ambiguous goal D. No support
**Q09 (O4)** "Disagree and commit" means: A. Silently comply B. Keep arguing C. Voice concerns, then fully support the decision D. Ignore the decision
**Q10 (O4)** Escalate a disagreement when: A. Immediately B. It is blocked or the risk is high, with a neutral summary C. You are losing D. Never
