# SRB-012 · Architecture governance — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 RFC process · O2 design reviews · O3 exceptions · O4 right-sizing.

**Q01 (O1)** An RFC reaches its comment deadline with open comments. What happens? A. The owner decides and records the outcome B. It waits indefinitely C. It is rejected D. It restarts
**Q02 (O1)** Where belongs the recommendation in an RFC meant for one review? A. At the end B. Near the top with the decision requested C. In a separate chat D. Omitted so reviewers decide
**Q03 (O2)** A reviewer adds a new, unrelated requirement at the end of a review. Best response? A. Block the decision B. Ignore silently C. Classify it as a suggestion or follow-up unless it is a real risk, and keep the date D. Cancel the review
**Q04 (O2)** A review's main output is: A. A decision with required changes and owners B. A longer document C. Reviewer approval stamps D. A new meeting series
**Q05 (O2)** A blocking concern is: A. Any disagreement B. A reviewer's preference C. A style issue D. A risk that must be fixed or explicitly accepted before proceeding
**Q06 (O3)** An exception should include: A. Only the reason B. Owner, risk, mitigation, and expiry C. Executive approval only D. A new standard
**Q07 (O3)** Many exceptions to one standard suggest: A. Teams are careless B. Exceptions are working as intended C. The standard may be wrong or hard to follow D. The process is too fast
**Q08 (O4)** Governance weight should depend mainly on: A. Team size B. Reversibility and blast radius C. Author seniority D. Tool in use
**Q09 (O4)** Which gets the heaviest process? A. Choosing a test helper library B. Renaming an internal class C. Tuning a retry timeout D. A public event contract consumed by several teams
**Q10 (O1)** A healthy sign for a governance process is: A. Decisions made by the stated date and rarely reopened B. Many meetings C. Reviewers never disagree D. All changes get the same review

## Answers
Q01 A · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 A · Q05 D · Q06 B · Q07 C · Q08 B · Q09 D · Q10 A

## Practical part
Facilitate a review of a peer's or role-player's proposal (SRB-012 assessment) and complete `SRB-012-LAB1`. Pass: at least 8/10 on the quiz, a review that ends in a recorded decision, and both lab RFCs decided within the deadline.
