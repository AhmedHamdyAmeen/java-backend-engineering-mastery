# SRB-020 · Stakeholder communication — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRB-020-LAB1 · Program communication
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3 h
1. Write two biweekly status reports for the FulfillCo program with defined status criteria, risks, and asks; the second includes an honest amber or red item.
2. Write a proposal to extend scope that a non-technical leader can approve.
3. Present to role-played executives and answer questions.
**Acceptance:** executives can state the decision asked of them after reading.

## Exercises (assessment: presentation and written report scored on the COM dimension)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRB-020-EX01 | 1 | Translate five technical facts about the FulfillCo integration into business impact statements with evidence. |
| SRB-020-EX02 | 1 | Write a one-paragraph explanation of event-driven stock sync for the finance director. |
| SRB-020-EX03 | 2 | Rewrite a green status report that hides a slipping dependency into an honest amber report. |
| SRB-020-EX04 | 2 | Define status criteria (green, amber, red) for the program in four lines. |
| SRB-020-EX05 | 3 | Draft a one-page proposal to extend scope to returns, with options, cost, and decision date. |
| SRB-020-EX06 | 4 | Write the opening paragraph and options for telling leadership the program is four weeks late. |
| SRB-020-EX07 | 4 | Case: you discover a data-loss risk two days before a demo to executives. Write what you say, to whom, and when. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Example: "20-minute status delay" becomes "more support contacts and refunds"; each has a measurable evidence source.
- **EX02** Avoid jargon: stock changes are sent as messages when they happen, so Shop shows accurate availability; cost and risk in a sentence each.
- **EX03** Name the slipping dependency, the effect on the date, the decision needed, and the date by which it is needed.
- **EX04** Green: on track, no milestone at risk. Amber: a milestone at risk without action. Red: a milestone will be missed without a decision.
- **EX05** Ask and recommendation first, then problem in business terms, options table, do-nothing consequence, timeline, appendix.
- **EX06** "The program will finish four weeks later than planned. Here are three options with their cost and customer impact; I recommend option B."
- **EX07** Tell the sponsor immediately with facts, impact, options (fix first, scope the demo, disclose), and your recommendation; agree an update time.
- Outcome 2 is also covered by the lab reports and Q04-Q05.

**Workplace evidence:** a proposal you wrote that was approved by non-engineering leadership.
