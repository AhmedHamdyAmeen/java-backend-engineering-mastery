# SRB-020 · Stakeholder communication — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 business impact · O2 status reports · O3 proposals · O4 bad news.

**Q01 (O1)** Which is a business impact lens? A. Code coverage B. Customer experience C. Build time D. Branch count
**Q02 (O1)** "Shipment status is delayed by 20 minutes" best translates to: A. More support contacts and refunds B. Higher CPU C. A lagging consumer D. A queue depth alert
**Q03 (O1)** When stating numbers for leaders you should: A. Hide assumptions B. Use exact figures only C. Give ranges and state assumptions D. Avoid numbers
**Q04 (O2)** A status report's colors are trustworthy when: A. They are always green B. Defined by criteria C. Chosen by mood D. Set by the executive
**Q05 (O2)** A risk should appear in the report: A. Only when red B. While it can still be addressed C. After it happens D. Never
**Q06 (O3)** In a proposal for executives the first element is: A. Architecture B. History C. The decision requested and the recommendation D. Appendix
**Q07 (O3)** Where does technical detail belong in a proposal for non-technical readers? A. In the appendix B. In the first paragraph C. Nowhere D. In the title
**Q08 (O3)** A good proposal also states: A. What happens if nothing is done B. Only benefits C. The team's opinion D. A glossary only
**Q09 (O4)** Best time to deliver bad news: A. After a fix B. At the end of the quarter C. As soon as you are reasonably sure, with options D. Never
**Q10 (O4)** A good opening for a four-week slip: A. A long history B. Blame of a dependency C. A joke D. The slip, its size, and that options follow

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 A · Q03 C · Q04 B · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 A · Q08 A · Q09 C · Q10 D
