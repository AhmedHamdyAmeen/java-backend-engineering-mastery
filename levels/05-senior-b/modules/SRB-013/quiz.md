# SRB-013 · Technology evaluation — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 framing and criteria · O2 proofs of concept · O3 matrices, total cost, exit cost · O4 bias.

**Q01 (O1)** The first step of an evaluation is: A. Contact vendors B. Write the problem, constraints, and success measures C. Build a prototype D. Pick a shortlist
**Q02 (O1)** Criteria weights should be agreed: A. After scoring B. After the PoC C. Before options are scored D. By the vendor
**Q03 (O2)** A good PoC primarily tests: A. The vendor's demo flow B. The riskiest assumptions, including failures C. Marketing claims D. UI polish
**Q04 (O2)** The PoC code should be: A. Promoted directly to production B. Hidden C. Copied into the main repo D. Treated as disposable or clearly labeled
**Q05 (O3)** In a scenario where the top option wins only if one weight stays high, you should: A. Report the winner B. Drop the weight C. Flag sensitivity and get the weight decided D. Pick the cheapest
**Q06 (O3)** Total cost over three years should include: A. Licence only B. Licence, infrastructure, engineering time, operations, and training C. Hosting only D. Nothing beyond purchase price
**Q07 (O4)** A vendor demo is risky evidence because it: A. Is always dishonest B. Shows the best path, not failures or your scale C. Is too long D. Needs travel
**Q08 (O4)** "We already spent six months on this tool" is an example of: A. Sunk cost bias B. Novelty bias C. Sensitivity analysis D. Exit planning
**Q09 (O3)** An exit plan should include: A. A legal threat B. A date to leave C. Triggers, data export path, rewrite effort, and a test of the export D. Nothing; exit is unlikely
**Q10 (O1)** Why score the "do nothing" option? A. It is cheaper B. Vendors require it C. It is always chosen D. It sets the baseline products must beat

Q01 B · Q02 C · Q03 B · Q04 D · Q05 C · Q06 B · Q07 B · Q08 A · Q09 C · Q10 D
