# SRB-011 · Cross-team design — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 ownership and dependency mapping · O2 inclusive design process · O3 contract-first parallel delivery · O4 conflict resolution.

**Q01 (O1)** Three teams all wait on one provider team's API. What is the best reading? A. That team is the critical path and needs contracts and unblocking first B. The three teams should merge C. The provider is badly managed D. Nothing, it is normal
**Q02 (O1)** A dependency map is complete for an interface when it has: A. A diagram B. A single consumer C. An owner and a date D. A Jira epic
**Q03 (O2)** You designed alone and present for sign-off. Teams raise big objections in the meeting. Most likely cause? A. Teams are difficult B. Objections arrive when change is expensive because teams were not included early C. The diagram was too large D. The meeting was too long
**Q04 (O2)** The best use of a design-session pre-read is to: A. Replace the meeting entirely B. Impress executives C. Let the session spend time on constraints and decisions D. Avoid recording decisions
**Q05 (O3)** A consumer team can start before the provider finishes by using: A. A stub derived from the agreed contract B. A direct database connection C. A copy of provider code D. A longer deadline
**Q06 (O3)** Contract tests primarily prevent: A. Slow builds B. Memory leaks C. Unreviewed code D. Provider and consumer drifting from the agreed schema
**Q07 (O3)** Feature flags help parallel delivery because they: A. Remove the need for contracts B. Let unfinished work be merged and deployed without being visible C. Speed up databases D. Replace testing
**Q08 (O4)** Two teams disagree on a design. What should you do first? A. Escalate immediately B. Choose the cheaper option C. Agree decision criteria, then score options against them D. Vote
**Q09 (O4)** When escalating a conflict, bring: A. A complaint about the other team B. Options with a recommendation and the criteria used C. Only the deadline D. Nothing until asked
**Q10 (O2)** A design session ends. What makes it effective? A. Everyone felt heard B. The slides were shared C. No one disagreed D. Decisions, owners, and dates are written and confirmed by teams

## Answers
Q01 A · Q02 C · Q03 B · Q04 C · Q05 A · Q06 D · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 B · Q10 D

## Practical part
Facilitate a recorded cross-team design session with role-played leads (SRB-011 assessment) and complete `SRB-011-LAB1`. Pass: at least 8/10 on the quiz, a facilitation score of at least the pass mark set by role-players, every interface with an owner and a date, and one conflict resolved on record.
