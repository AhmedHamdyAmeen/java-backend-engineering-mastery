# SRB-018 · Mentoring senior peers — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 facilitating design discussions · O2 resolving conflict · O3 influence without authority · O4 peer feedback.

**Q01 (O1)** To keep the loudest voice from anchoring a group, you should: A. Speak first B. Limit the meeting C. Collect written positions before discussion D. Skip criteria
**Q02 (O1)** A design discussion should end with: A. Agreement to continue B. A decision or dated experiment with an owner C. Silence D. A longer document
**Q03 (O2)** Two seniors dispute a design. A first useful step is: A. Choose your favorite B. Escalate at once C. Wait D. Identify whether the dispute is facts, values, or priorities
**Q04 (O2)** Turning a disputed opinion into a testable claim lets you: A. Settle it with evidence or an experiment B. Win the argument C. Avoid the decision D. Delay the project
**Q05 (O2)** "Disagree and commit" means: A. Silence B. Everyone must agree C. Support the decision after it is made, with dissent recorded D. The loudest wins
**Q06 (O3)** Without authority, the best first move to influence is: A. Escalate B. Understand what others care about C. Present slides D. Wait for approval
**Q07 (O3)** Which builds shared ownership of a proposal? A. Involving people early B. Announcing it C. Sending it late D. Making it mandatory
**Q08 (O4)** Actionable feedback includes: A. A label of the person's character B. A comparison with others C. Observed behavior, its effect, and a concrete next step D. Only praise
**Q09 (O4)** You start hard feedback by: A. Listing all faults B. Copying their manager C. Posting in a channel D. Stating the purpose and asking their view
**Q10 (O1)** Mentoring a peer differs from mentoring a junior because you: A. Help them think and decide rather than telling them B. Give more instructions C. Avoid feedback D. Decide for them

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 C · Q06 B · Q07 A · Q08 C · Q09 D · Q10 A

## Practical part
Complete the facilitation simulation (`SRB-018-LAB1`, SRB-018 assessment) scored by role-players and an observer. Pass: at least 8/10 on the quiz and a facilitation score at or above the pass mark, ending in a decision both role-played engineers commit to.
