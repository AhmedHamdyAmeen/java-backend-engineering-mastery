# SRB-018 · Mentoring senior peers — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRB-018-LAB1 · Deadlock
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3 h
Two role-played senior engineers disagree on the stock-ledger design (for example a single ledger with strong consistency versus per-warehouse ledgers with reconciliation). Meet each, facilitate a joint session with agreed criteria, and bring them to a decision both commit to. Finish with brief feedback to one of them.
**Acceptance:** scored by role-players and an observer.

## Exercises (assessment: facilitation simulation)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRB-018-EX01 | 1 | Case: a senior peer dominates every design discussion. Write your facilitation plan for the next one. |
| SRB-018-EX02 | 1 | Prepare a 20-minute facilitation outline for choosing between two stock-ledger designs. |
| SRB-018-EX03 | 2 | Complete a conflict worksheet for two peers disagreeing on synchronous versus asynchronous stock updates. |
| SRB-018-EX04 | 2 | Case: after a decision, one engineer keeps reopening it. Write what you say and what you record. |
| SRB-018-EX05 | 3 | Build an influence map for proposing a shared event-schema standard to three team leads with different concerns. |
| SRB-018-EX06 | 4 | Write feedback for a senior peer who merges changes without review, using the observation-effect-next-step form. |
| SRB-018-EX07 | 4 | Review a draft piece of feedback ("you are too negative in reviews") and rewrite it so it can be acted on. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Collect written positions first, use a speaking order, restate positions, ask quieter voices directly, decide by criteria; speak privately with the dominant peer afterwards.
- **EX02** Frame, written positions, steelman, score on criteria, then decision or experiment, with a decider named.
- **EX03** Claims, underlying concerns (latency, consistency risk), evidence to gather (load test, failure drill), decider, review date.
- **EX04** Restate the decision and the recorded dissent; ask what new evidence exists; if none, ask for commitment, if some, agree a review trigger.
- **EX05** Rows per lead: what they care about, concern, what would help, who they trust; plan early involvement and a small reversible pilot.
- **EX06** "In the last two releases you merged without review; the effect was a defect found in production. What was the reason? What could we agree for urgent changes?"
- **EX07** Replace the label with dated examples, effect on others, and a concrete request such as "start with the question before the objection".
- Outcome 3 is also covered by the lab observer scoring.

**Workplace evidence:** a disagreement between senior engineers you helped resolve.
