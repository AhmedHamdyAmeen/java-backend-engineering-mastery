# SRB-014 · Engineering metrics — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 DORA metrics · O2 delivery plus reliability · O3 avoiding backfire · O4 metric to experiment.

**Q01 (O1)** Lead time for changes measures: A. Time from commit to running in production B. Time to write code C. Sprint length D. Time between incidents
**Q02 (O1)** Change failure rate is: A. Bugs per line B. Test failures per build C. Share of releases that cause a failure needing a fix or rollback D. Incidents per month
**Q03 (O1)** Which two DORA measures reflect stability? A. Frequency and lead time B. Lead time and story points C. Velocity and burndown D. Change failure rate and time to restore
**Q04 (O2)** Deployment frequency rises while SLO attainment falls. Best reading? A. Great progress B. Speed may be coming at the expense of reliability C. SLOs are wrong D. Metrics are broken
**Q05 (O3)** Goodhart's law says: A. Measure more B. Teams must be ranked C. A measure used as a target stops being a good measure D. Metrics are useless
**Q06 (O3)** A team splits one change into many tiny deploys to raise frequency. That is: A. Good practice B. Gaming C. Required by DORA D. A failure metric
**Q07 (O3)** Best way to use delivery metrics across teams: A. Rank teams B. Publish league tables C. Pay bonuses on them D. Track each team's trend against its own baseline with context
**Q08 (O4)** A valid improvement experiment needs: A. A baseline, a measure, and a decision rule B. A new tool C. A large budget D. Agreement from all teams
**Q09 (O4)** Why use the 85th percentile of lead time as well as the median? A. It is always lower B. Outliers show the slow tail teams experience C. It is easier D. DORA requires it
**Q10 (O2)** Error budget burn tells you: A. How many bugs exist B. How many deploys remain C. How fast the allowed unreliability is being used D. Team velocity

## Answers
Q01 A · Q02 C · Q03 D · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 B · Q07 D · Q08 A · Q09 B · Q10 C

## Practical part
Submit a metrics narrative for the program dashboard (SRB-014 assessment) and complete `SRB-014-LAB1`. Pass: at least 8/10 on the quiz, a narrative that pairs delivery and reliability data, and a recorded experiment with hypothesis, measure, and result.
