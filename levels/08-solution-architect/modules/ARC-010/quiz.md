# ARC-010 · Legacy modernization — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 disposition options · O2 portfolio assessment · O3 sequencing and coexistence · O4 business case.

**Q01 (O1)** Moving an application to new infrastructure with no code change is: A. Refactor B. Replace C. Rehost D. Retire
**Q02 (O1)** Switching off an application nobody needs is: A. Retire B. Replatform C. Retain D. Rehost
**Q03 (O1)** Which is a weak reason to modernize? A. Blocks business change B. Out of vendor support with security risk C. Very high run cost D. It is old and unfashionable
**Q04 (O2)** A high-value, low-condition application suggests: A. Retire it B. Ignore it C. Invest through refactor or replace D. Rehost and stop
**Q05 (O2)** Which is NOT a normal portfolio assessment criterion? A. Business value B. Technical condition C. Risk D. Developer preference for a language
**Q06 (O2)** Why base scores on evidence? A. It shortens meetings B. Opinion favors disliked systems; evidence is defensible C. Evidence is optional D. It hides disagreements
**Q07 (O3)** The strangler approach: A. Replaces everything overnight B. Freezes the old system C. Routes slices to the new system while the old shrinks D. Avoids data sync
**Q08 (O3)** During coexistence you must define: A. System of record per entity B. Office layout C. Brand colors D. Nothing special
**Q09 (O4)** A "do nothing" option in a business case is: A. Forbidden B. A baseline for comparison C. A trick D. Only for small programs
**Q10 (O4)** A responsible benefits estimate: A. Credits all growth to the new system B. Omits costs C. Uses ranges, assumptions, and attributable benefits D. Is a single number

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 A · Q03 D · Q04 C · Q05 D · Q06 B · Q07 C · Q08 A · Q09 B · Q10 C

## Practical part
Disposition defense using the lab evidence sheet and decisions plus exercises `ARC-010-EX01` to `EX06`. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least five of six exercises correct, and each disposition justified by value, condition, and risk.
