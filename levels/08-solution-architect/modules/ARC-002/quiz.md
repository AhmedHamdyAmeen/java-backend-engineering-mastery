# ARC-002 · Business architecture — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 capability maps and maturity · O2 value streams and processes · O3 stakeholder mapping · O4 traceability to the solution.

**Q01 (O1)** Which is a business capability? A. Order Management B. Finance department C. The ERP D. The web team
**Q02 (O1)** A heat map of maturity and importance mainly helps to: A. Choose a programming language B. Name the product C. Find where investment matters most D. Draw network diagrams
**Q03 (O1)** Which capability is the strongest investment candidate? A. High importance, high maturity B. Low importance, high maturity C. Low importance, low maturity D. High importance, low maturity
**Q04 (O1)** A capability map that mirrors the org chart is a problem because: A. Capabilities should be stable and independent of structure B. It is too small C. It lacks colors D. It cannot be printed
**Q05 (O2)** A value stream is named from the point of view of: A. The IT department B. The stakeholder receiving value C. The vendor D. The database
**Q06 (O2)** Which should be agreed first? A. Detailed process steps B. Class diagrams C. The value stream D. Code structure
**Q07 (O2)** Return-to-Refund and Order-to-Delivery in Larkspur: A. Never share capabilities B. Share some capabilities, such as Payments and Inventory C. Are the same stream D. Are processes inside one system
**Q08 (O3)** A high-influence, low-interest stakeholder should be: A. Ignored B. Kept informed only C. Kept satisfied D. Managed as a delivery team
**Q09 (O3)** Why include store managers in the stakeholder map? A. They approve budgets B. They absorb change and know real pain points C. They choose vendors D. They own the ERP
**Q10 (O4)** A proposed component that traces to no capability suggests: A. It must be built first B. It is an enterprise standard C. It is already built D. Unnecessary scope or a missed need
