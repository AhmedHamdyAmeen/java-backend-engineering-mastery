# ARC-002 · Business architecture — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### ARC-002-LAB1 · Larkspur business architecture
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3.5 h
1. From the case brief and interview notes, build the capability map: L1 domains and L2 capabilities, named consistently.
2. Rate each capability for maturity and strategic importance with a one-line evidence note; produce the heat map.
3. Model two value streams, order-to-delivery and return-to-refund, with stages, capabilities, and value added.
4. Build the stakeholder map (influence and interest) with goals and concerns per stakeholder.
5. Trace the proposed solution components to capabilities and stages.
6. Present to role-played business owners and adjust.
**Acceptance:** role-played business owners agree the heat map reflects their pain points.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)

| ARC-002-EX04 | 2 | A teammate has modeled order picking in 40 BPMN steps before agreeing the stream. Explain the problem and propose the next step. |
| ARC-002-EX05 | 3 | Plot ten Larkspur stakeholders on influence/interest and write the engagement approach for the three most critical. |
| ARC-002-EX06 | 4 | Trace "customer changes a pickup slot" to capabilities and components and list any gaps. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Examples: Manage Store Operations, Manage Customer Orders, Manage Inventory, Process Payments, Handle Customer Inquiries, Produce Management Reports. Capabilities are not departments.
- **EX02** Investment candidates are those with high importance and low maturity, such as Inventory Visibility and Click-and-Collect Fulfillment, supported by evidence such as stock accuracy figures.
- **EX03** Stages: Request return, Authorize, Receive item, Inspect, Refund, Restock. Capabilities include Customer Service, Returns Management, Payments, Inventory.
- **EX04** Process detail was modeled before agreeing the value stream; step back, agree the stream and its stages, and detail only the processes that matter for the change.
- **EX05** CFO: keep satisfied; head of e-commerce: manage closely; store managers: keep informed and involve early because they absorb change.
- **EX06** Capabilities: Order Orchestration, Store Fulfillment, Customer Communication; components: order service, store app, notification service; gap: slot management has no current owner or system.

Outcomes 1 and 3 are also covered by the lab and the capability map defense.

**Workplace evidence:** a capability map or value-stream model used to scope a real initiative.
