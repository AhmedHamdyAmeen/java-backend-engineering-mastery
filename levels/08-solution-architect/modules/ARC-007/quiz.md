# ARC-007 · Architecture principles — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 writing principles · O2 applying principle families · O3 resolving conflicts · O4 evaluating options and exceptions.

**Q01 (O1)** Which is a usable principle? A. "Use best practices" B. "Be agile" C. "Do the right thing" D. "Other systems access customer data only through its owning API"
**Q02 (O1)** The implications section of a principle states: A. The author's name B. What we must do or accept as a result C. The history D. The product list
**Q03 (O1)** A principle is not a platitude if: A. It is short B. It is in the framework C. Someone could reasonably argue the opposite D. It is long
**Q04 (O2)** "Integrate through published interfaces" is mainly a principle of: A. Interoperability B. Cost C. Branding D. Hosting
**Q05 (O2)** Putting the ERP behind an anti-corruption layer serves mainly: A. Evolvability B. Color C. Licensing D. Training
**Q06 (O2)** "Card data is tokenized and never stored in the shop" is a: A. Standardization principle B. Evolvability principle C. Security principle D. Data warehouse rule
**Q07 (O3)** When two principles conflict you should: A. Ignore one silently B. Choose by context and record the reasoning C. Delete both D. Wait
**Q08 (O3)** Frequent exceptions to one principle suggest: A. Teams are lazy B. The principle may need revision C. The principle is perfect D. Exceptions should be hidden
**Q09 (O4)** A sound exception has: A. An owner, a rationale, and an expiry or review date B. No record C. A verbal approval D. A vendor name
**Q10 (O4)** Principles help evaluate options by: A. Replacing requirements B. Choosing the vendor C. Providing criteria to compare how well options follow them D. Setting prices

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 A · Q05 A · Q06 C · Q07 B · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Principles review of the lab set and exercises `ARC-007-EX01` to `EX06`. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least five of six exercises correct, and a principle set where at least one principle changed a decision and one conflict is resolved on record.
