# ARC-003 · Requirements discovery — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 workshops and interviews · O2 functional and nonfunctional capture · O3 constraints and assumptions · O4 conflicts and trade-offs · O5 baseline and traceability.

**Q01 (O1)** A workshop beats separate interviews when: A. Views are private B. Nobody is available C. Participants must agree or surface conflicts together D. Only one person decides
**Q02 (O1)** Which question best uncovers priorities? A. "What do you want?" B. "What happens if this fails at noon on a sale day?" C. "Are you happy?" D. "Any other ideas?"
**Q03 (O2)** A well-formed nonfunctional requirement includes: A. A framework name B. A color C. A product name D. A measure under stated conditions
**Q04 (O2)** "The shop must be secure" is best improved by: A. Naming a threat and the expected response B. Adding "very" C. Removing it D. Moving it to design
**Q05 (O2)** Failure flows in a journey are important because: A. They shorten the document B. Real risk and design drivers live there C. They replace tests D. They are optional by definition
**Q06 (O3)** An unverified belief about peak traffic is a(n): A. Constraint B. Requirement C. Assumption D. Decision
**Q07 (O3)** Which is a constraint? A. "Stores probably have fast networks" B. "Users like blue" C. "Peak will be 3x" D. "Payment data may not leave the approved region"
**Q08 (O4)** When two requirements conflict, the architect should: A. Pick the cheaper silently B. Present options and consequences to the accountable owner C. Ask the loudest stakeholder D. Delay until build
**Q09 (O4)** Why look beneath a stated demand? A. To avoid meetings B. To lengthen discovery C. Needs can be met by options the demand excludes D. To please the sponsor
**Q10 (O5)** A traceability ID per requirement mainly supports: A. Faster compile times B. Change control and coverage checks C. Marketing D. Code style

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 D · Q08 B · Q09 C · Q10 B

## Practical part
Workshop facilitation scored by role-players using the lab, plus baseline review and exercises `ARC-003-EX01` to `EX06`. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least five of six exercises correct, and a baseline where every nonfunctional requirement is measurable and every conflict has an owner.
