# ARC-041 · Stakeholder management — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 stakeholder mapping · O2 tailored engagement · O3 resolving conflicts · O4 keeping alignment.

**Q01 (O1)** A CISO with power to block go-live but little day-to-day involvement sits in which place on the map? A. High influence, lower interest: keep satisfied and engage early B. Low influence: monitor only C. Ignore until testing D. Same as a store manager
**Q02 (O1)** Your project plan lists only the sponsor and the e-commerce team. The main risk is: A. Too many meetings B. Too few diagrams C. Unengaged stakeholders (security, operations, stores, vendors) blocking late D. The sponsor leaving
**Q03 (O2)** Which message suits the head of IT operations most? A. Customer conversion forecasts B. Run impact, monitoring, support model, and acceptance criteria C. Colour palette of the UI D. Sprint velocity
**Q04 (O2)** A skeptical, influential store operations head should be: A. First met in the steering group B. Left out to avoid delay C. Engaged one-to-one early to hear concerns before the design is fixed D. Sent only emails
**Q05 (O3)** E-commerce wants speed and security wants control. Your first step is to: A. Pick the louder side B. Restate each side's underlying goal and look for options C. Escalate immediately D. Postpone the decision
**Q06 (O3)** Who decides a trade-off between two stakeholders? A. The architect alone B. The most senior attendee in the room C. The named accountable decision owner, with the architect facilitating D. A vote of all attendees
**Q07 (O3)** The best way to record a resolved conflict is: A. Verbal agreement only B. A chat message C. In the architect's notebook D. A decision log or ADR entry with trade-off, owner, and review date
**Q08 (O4)** A decision is challenged months later by a new manager without new facts. You: A. Reverse it B. Share the log and rationale, and reopen only if the basis has changed C. Refuse to discuss D. Ask the sponsor to overrule
**Q09 (O4)** Which practice best sustains alignment through delivery? A. Regular touchpoints with a "what changed" item and a visible decision log B. One kickoff meeting C. Monthly emails with no discussion D. Meeting only at go-live
**Q10 (O4)** You learn of a significant delay. You should: A. Wait for the next steering date B. Fix it quietly C. Tell affected stakeholders promptly with options and impacts D. Only tell the sponsor after recovery

## Answers
Q01 A · Q02 C · Q03 B · Q04 C · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 D · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Stakeholder simulation on the Larkspur lab: a map, an engagement plan, and a recorded conflict resolution with a documented trade-off and decision owner. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least four of five exercises correct, and a lab with an explicit decision owner and recorded trade-off.
