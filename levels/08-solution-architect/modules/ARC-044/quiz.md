# ARC-044 · Architecture communication — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 executive presentation · O2 engineering presentation · O3 defending a design · O4 diagrams and narratives per audience.

**Q01 (O1)** You have ten minutes with the Larkspur steering committee. The strongest opening is: A. The ask, with the business reason B. A history of the old web shop C. The service catalog D. Your team's tooling
**Q02 (O1)** Which slide best suits executives? A. A sequence diagram of events B. Recommendation, first-value date, cost and benefit with assumptions, and top risks C. API error codes D. A deployment YAML
**Q03 (O1)** After the executive talk, success means executives can: A. Draw the architecture B. List the technology stack C. Name the vendors D. State the decision asked of them and why
**Q04 (O2)** For an engineering team, the most valuable content beyond the structure is: A. Budget approvals B. Interface contracts, failure behaviour, and the reasons for decisions and rejected alternatives C. Marketing slides D. Org charts only
**Q05 (O2)** Telling a team what is fixed and what they may decide: A. Reduces autonomy for no reason B. Is unnecessary C. Prevents inconsistency while keeping local freedom D. Replaces documentation
**Q06 (O3)** A panel claims your stock sync could oversell. The best reply is: A. "That will not happen" B. "That is the vendor's problem" C. "We have always done it this way" D. State what is measured, what is assumed, and the trade-off, then offer a follow-up test
**Q07 (O3)** You do not know the answer to a challenge. You should: A. Guess confidently B. Say so, state what you will check, and give a date C. Change the subject D. Defer to the most senior person
**Q08 (O3)** A challenger is right about a missing retry behaviour. You should: A. Deny it B. Hide it in the appendix C. Concede, record the change, and update the design D. Ask them to put it in writing only
**Q09 (O4)** Which diagram best helps store operations leads? A. A process flow showing what changes in their daily work B. A network packet trace C. A class diagram D. A database schema
**Q10 (O4)** Keeping one architecture pack as the source of truth for all presentations ensures: A. Slides are never needed B. All audiences hear the same facts at different depth C. Presentations are longer D. Engineers need not present

## Answers
Q01 A · Q02 B · Q03 D · Q04 B · Q05 C · Q06 D · Q07 B · Q08 C · Q09 A · Q10 B

## Practical part
Presentation and defense scored on the COM dimension: the ten-minute executive talk and the 30-minute technical defense on the Larkspur lab. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least four of five exercises correct, and a lab where executives can state the ask and every challenge is answered or logged.
