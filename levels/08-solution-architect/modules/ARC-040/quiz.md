# ARC-040 · Vendor and procurement — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 technical RFI and RFP sections · O2 technical evaluation · O3 SLAs and contracts · O4 working with procurement and legal.

**Q01 (O1)** A good RFP requirement describes: A. A brand B. A product C. A price D. A need with measurable criteria
**Q02 (O1)** The RFI differs from the RFP in that the RFI: A. Binds the vendor to a price B. Explores the market and what exists C. Replaces the contract D. Is secret
**Q03 (O1)** Requirements marked mandatory versus desirable help: A. Avoid scoring B. Hide the weights C. Separate must-haves from scored extras D. Shorten the contract
**Q04 (O2)** Scorecard weights should be set: A. After reading responses B. After the demo C. After the favorite is chosen D. Before reading responses
**Q05 (O2)** Scripted demos mainly: A. Let all vendors show the same Larkspur scenarios B. Save time for vendors C. Remove the need for evidence D. Replace references
**Q06 (O2)** A proof of concept should target: A. The easiest feature B. The riskiest unknowns C. The logo D. The pricing page
**Q07 (O3)** Service credits in an SLA usually: A. Cover business loss fully B. Are huge C. Are small compared with the business loss D. Prevent outages
**Q08 (O3)** Which clause is most important to avoid lock-in? A. Payment terms B. Notice period for invoices C. Marketing rights D. Data return in an open format and exit assistance
**Q09 (O4)** In procurement the architect acts as: A. The negotiator for price B. The legal signatory C. A technical advisor to procurement and legal D. The vendor's contact
**Q10 (O4)** Legal removes the exit clause to speed things up. The architect should: A. Agree silently B. Explain the architectural risk and propose a lighter explicit version C. Leave the project D. Add more features

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 D · Q05 A · Q06 B · Q07 C · Q08 D · Q09 C · Q10 B

## Practical part
RFP and evaluation review of the lab artifacts plus exercises `ARC-040-EX01` to `EX06`. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least five of six exercises correct, and a lab with a scorecard traceable to requirements and exit risks identified in the sample contract.
