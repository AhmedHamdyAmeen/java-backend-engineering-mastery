# ARC-022 · Cloud architecture frameworks — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 pillars · O2 running and prioritizing a review · O3 remediation.

**Q01 (O1)** Which pillar covers recovering from failure? A. Cost optimization B. Sustainability C. Reliability D. Performance efficiency
**Q02 (O1)** Which pillar covers running, monitoring, and improving through automation? A. Operational excellence B. Security C. Reliability D. Sustainability
**Q03 (O1)** Reducing the environmental impact of a workload belongs to: A. Security B. Cost optimization C. Reliability D. Sustainability
**Q04 (O1)** Using resources well as demand changes belongs to: A. Performance efficiency B. Security C. Operational excellence D. Cost optimization only
**Q05 (O2)** In a review, you should ask for: A. Opinions only B. Verbal promises C. Evidence such as configurations and test results D. Marketing slides
**Q06 (O2)** Findings should be prioritized by: A. Ease of fixing B. Number per pillar C. Alphabetical order D. Risk: likelihood and impact
**Q07 (O2)** Fixing low-risk findings first is: A. A common mistake B. Best practice C. Required D. A tie-breaker only
**Q08 (O3)** Each finding in a remediation plan needs: A. An owner, a date, and a success measure B. Only a title C. A logo D. No owner
**Q09 (O3)** An accepted risk must record: A. Nothing B. The architect's name only C. The sponsor's logo D. A named owner who accepted it and a review date
**Q10 (O3)** The review should be: A. A one-time checkbox B. Repeated after major changes C. Only for audits D. Skipped for new designs

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 A · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 C · Q06 D · Q07 A · Q08 A · Q09 D · Q10 B

Review report using the `ARC-022-LAB1` findings and remediation plan plus exercises `ARC-022-EX01` to `EX06`. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least five of six exercises correct, and every high-risk finding fixed in the design or accepted by a named owner.
