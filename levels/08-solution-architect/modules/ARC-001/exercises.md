# ARC-001 · Architecture roles — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### ARC-001-LAB1 · Larkspur engagement charter
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~2.5 h
1. List the stakeholders and roles around the Larkspur program: sponsor, store operations, e-commerce, finance, IT, enterprise architecture, delivery teams, vendors.
2. Write the scope of the solution architect's engagement: in scope, out of scope, deliverables.
3. Add a decision-rights table (recommends, decides, consulted) for at least five decisions.
4. Define interfaces with enterprise architecture, delivery teams, and vendors: cadence, inputs, outputs.
5. Put it on one page and review it with a role-played program sponsor.
**Acceptance:** reviewed by a role-played program sponsor; no overlap or gap with other roles.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ARC-001-EX01 | 1 | Given eight Larkspur work items, assign each to software, solution, enterprise, cloud, or data architect and justify by scope and horizon. |
| ARC-001-EX02 | 1 | A candidate says "I am a solution architect; I own the capability roadmap and the cloud landing zone." Explain what is wrong. |
| ARC-001-EX03 | 2 | Draw where the Larkspur solution architect sits relative to sponsor, EA, three delivery teams, and the commerce vendor; label what flows each way. |
| ARC-001-EX04 | 3 | List five accountabilities and five non-accountabilities for the Larkspur program. |
| ARC-001-EX05 | 3 | The sponsor asks you to set the delivery date and review each pull request. Write a polite reply that restores boundaries. |
| ARC-001-EX06 | 4 | Write a one-page transition plan from your current role to solution architect: gaps, evidence, three actions with dates. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Examples: web shop module structure → software; end-to-end commerce design → solution; three-year retail capability roadmap → enterprise; landing zone and network → cloud; customer master data model → data. Judge by scope and horizon.
- **EX02** The roadmap is enterprise architecture's; the landing zone is the cloud architect's. The solution architect applies and consumes both and may propose changes through the proper channel.
- **EX03** Sponsor: outcomes and budget in, options and recommendations out. EA: principles and standards in, exceptions and feedback out. Teams: guidance out, feedback in. Vendor: requirements out, product constraints in.
- **EX04** Accountable: solution fitness, options, decision log, architectural risks, quality attribute coverage. Not: priorities and budget, enterprise standards, schedule, code quality inside teams, vendor contracts.
- **EX05** Offer to advise on the effects of dates on design risk, and to review architecturally significant changes through the decision log; the project manager owns the date and tech leads own pull requests.
- **EX06** A good plan names concrete evidence (a decision record, a facilitated workshop, a reviewed design) and dated actions rather than "learn architecture".

Outcome 2 is also covered by the lab's interface section; outcome 4 by the quiz.

**Workplace evidence:** a program where you held a defined architecture role with written scope and decision rights.
