# ARC-001 · Architecture roles — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 role distinctions · O2 position in a program · O3 accountabilities and boundaries · O4 transition from engineer.

**Q01 (O1)** Which role mainly owns the structure of a single product's code base? A. Enterprise architect B. Software architect C. Data architect D. Cloud architect
**Q02 (O1)** Larkspur needs a three-year capability roadmap for all business units. That is most like the work of the: A. Enterprise architect B. Solution architect C. Software architect D. Cloud architect
**Q03 (O1)** A solution architect's horizon is typically: A. A single sprint B. A decade C. The life of a program D. One code review
**Q04 (O2)** Who sets organization-wide architecture standards? A. Vendor B. Delivery team C. Enterprise architecture D. Solution architect alone
**Q05 (O2)** The solution architect's relationship to delivery teams is best described as: A. Guiding and unblocking while keeping the design coherent B. Writing all their code C. Approving every commit D. Having no contact after design
**Q06 (O3)** Which is NOT normally a solution architect's accountability? A. Decision log B. Quality attribute coverage C. Setting the program budget D. Architectural risks
**Q07 (O3)** The sponsor asks you to choose which store region launches first. This is a: A. Business decision you advise on B. Decision you own C. Enterprise architecture decision D. Vendor decision
**Q08 (O3)** Why write decision rights down? A. To prevent gaps and turf wars B. To satisfy the framework C. To avoid meetings D. To replace diagrams
**Q09 (O4)** A common mistake of a new solution architect is to: A. Facilitate workshops B. Write decision records C. Act as the lead developer D. Review options
**Q10 (O4)** Solution architect and Staff engineer are: A. Two steps on one ladder B. Identical titles C. The same as enterprise architect D. Parallel, related tracks

## Answers
Q01 B · Q02 A · Q03 C · Q04 C · Q05 A · Q06 C · Q07 A · Q08 A · Q09 C · Q10 D

## Practical part
Charter review by a role-played sponsor plus the role-scenario exercises `ARC-001-EX01` to `EX06`. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least five of six exercises correct, and a charter with no role overlap or gap.
