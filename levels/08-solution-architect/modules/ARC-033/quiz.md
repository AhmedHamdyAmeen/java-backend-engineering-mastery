# ARC-033 · Solution options — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 generate real options · O2 criteria and weights · O3 decision matrix and sensitivity · O4 recommend and record rejections.

**Q01 (O1)** Which set of Larkspur options differs in approach rather than in vendor? A. SaaS vendor 1, SaaS vendor 2, SaaS vendor 3 B. Platform X version 1, 2, 3 C. Three hosting regions D. Commerce SaaS, packaged platform, custom build, hybrid
**Q02 (O1)** A strawman option is: A. A baseline of keeping the current system B. A hybrid option C. An option with high risk D. An option written weakly so the favorite wins
**Q03 (O2)** When should evaluation weights be agreed? A. After scoring B. After the vendor demo C. Before scoring D. After approval
**Q04 (O2)** A must-have such as card data staying out of Larkspur's scope is best treated as: A. A pass/fail gate B. A heavily weighted preference C. A tie-breaker D. A risk note only
**Q05 (O3)** Two options total 3.9 and 3.8. The most defensible reading is: A. The first option wins clearly B. The second option is invalid C. Scoring is finished D. The options are close and weights matter
**Q06 (O3)** Sensitivity analysis checks: A. The speed of the servers B. Whether the winner changes when weights change C. Whether the team is happy D. The quality of the slides
**Q07 (O3)** Raising "speed to market" by 10 points flips the winner. You should: A. Hide it B. Delete the criterion C. Add more options D. Present the flip and ask the sponsor to rule on that weight
**Q08 (O4)** For rejected options, the recommendation page should record: A. Their honest strengths and why they lost B. Only their weaknesses C. Nothing D. The vendor's pricing only
**Q09 (O2)** The e-commerce and finance leads disagree on whether cost or speed matters more. The best move is: A. Choose the sponsor's preference silently B. Skip the criterion C. Score both ways and pick randomly D. Settle weights together in a workshop and record them
**Q10 (O4)** A good recommendation includes: A. Only the winning option B. The winner, the rejected options with strengths, and a re-evaluation trigger C. A list of vendors D. A project plan

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 D · Q03 C · Q04 A · Q05 D · Q06 B · Q07 D · Q08 A · Q09 D · Q10 B

## Practical part
Options defense with assessor challenges on the Larkspur lab: the assessor challenges weights, scores, and one rejected option. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least five of six exercises correct, and a lab with at least three real options, pre-agreed weights, a sensitivity table, and documented rejected-option strengths.
