# ARC-027 · Identity and access — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 RBAC and ABAC · O2 identity federation · O3 privileged access · O4 joiners, movers, leavers, and reviews.

**Q01 (O1)** "A store manager sees returns only for their own store" is best expressed as: A. 120 separate roles B. A shared account C. One role plus a store attribute check D. No control
**Q02 (O1)** Role explosion is caused by: A. Too few users B. Combining scope and permission into separate roles C. Using attributes D. Federation
**Q03 (O2)** Federation means: A. One system trusts another's login B. Copying passwords C. Shared databases D. Removing authentication
**Q04 (O2)** Which standards commonly support federation? A. FTP and Telnet B. OpenID Connect and SAML C. SMTP only D. SNMP
**Q05 (O2)** Why separate customer identities from workforce identities? A. Different risks, scale, and lifecycles B. Fewer logins C. Cheaper D. It is not needed
**Q06 (O3)** Just-in-time privileged access means: A. Permanent admin rights B. Rights granted when needed and expiring C. Shared passwords D. No review
**Q07 (O3)** A break-glass account should be: A. Used daily B. Shared openly C. Without logs D. Sealed, monitored, and reviewed after use
**Q08 (O4)** What does a mover process most often miss? A. Adding new access B. Training C. Removing the old role's access D. Welcome email
**Q09 (O4)** Access reviews exist so that: A. Owners confirm who still needs access and unneeded access is removed B. Users pick roles C. Logs get shorter D. Admins gain rights
**Q10 (O3)** A shared admin account is a problem mainly because: A. It is slow B. It costs more C. It needs more storage D. Actions cannot be traced to a person

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 A · Q04 B · Q05 A · Q06 B · Q07 D · Q08 C · Q09 A · Q10 D

## Practical part
Access model review: demonstrate least privilege for five representative Larkspur users in front of a reviewer. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, all five exercises correct, and a model with no shared admin accounts and a defined review cycle.
