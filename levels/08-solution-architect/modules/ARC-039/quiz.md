# ARC-039 · Financial analysis — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 total cost of ownership · O2 ROI, payback, and benefits · O3 capex and opex · O4 verifiable cost-benefit presentation.

**Q01 (O1)** TCO should include: A. Licences only B. Build only C. Build, run, licences, support, people, and exit over several years D. Nothing hidden
**Q02 (O1)** Comparing a SaaS option against zero cost for the current system is: A. Correct B. A common mistake because the current system has costs too C. Required D. Required by finance
**Q03 (O1)** A cost that often grows with volume is: A. Fixed rent B. A one-time workshop C. Per-transaction licences D. A paid-up licence
**Q04 (O2)** A benefit with no owner is: A. Safe B. Free C. Unlikely to be delivered or measured D. More likely to be delivered
**Q05 (O2)** Payback is the point where: A. The project starts B. Cumulative benefit equals cumulative cost C. The vendor is paid D. The budget resets
**Q06 (O2)** Sensitivity analysis answers: A. What colors to use B. Who owns the project C. Which vendor is largest D. How results change if key assumptions are wrong
**Q07 (O3)** A subscription option mostly shifts cost toward: A. Opex B. Capex C. Depreciation only D. No cost
**Q08 (O3)** Capex or opex treatment is finally decided by: A. The architect B. The vendor C. Finance D. The CIO's assistant
**Q09 (O4)** Finance can verify a business case when: A. Numbers are rounded B. Assumptions are hidden C. Only the total is shown D. Every number is traceable to an assumption and a source
**Q10 (O4)** A benefit such as "better customer experience" should be: A. Left as is B. Translated into a measurable effect with owner, or marked non-financial C. Removed silently D. Doubled

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 C · Q04 C · Q05 B · Q06 D · Q07 A · Q08 C · Q09 D · Q10 B
