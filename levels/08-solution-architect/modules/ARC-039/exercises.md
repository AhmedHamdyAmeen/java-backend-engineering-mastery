# ARC-039 · Financial analysis — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### ARC-039-LAB1 · Larkspur business case
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~4 h
1. Build a five-year TCO for the top two options with every cost line and assumption.
2. List benefits with owners, measures, baselines, and targets.
3. Calculate ROI and payback; show capex and opex profiles per option.
4. Run sensitivity on key assumptions (volume, delay, adoption, cost).
5. Present to a role-played finance partner who tries to reproduce the numbers.
**Acceptance:** a role-played finance partner can reproduce every number.

## Exercises (case-based; six exercises)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ARC-039-EX01 | 1 | Build a TCO outline for a commerce SaaS option: list at least twelve cost lines for years 0-5 including hidden costs. |
| ARC-039-EX02 | 1 | The sponsor says "SaaS is cheaper because there are no servers". Respond with the missing cost lines on both sides. |
| ARC-039-EX03 | 2 | Given investment 3.0M, annual benefit 1.4M from year 2 and 0.6M in year 1, compute simple payback and say which assumptions to test. |
| ARC-039-EX04 | 2 | Three benefits have no owners: faster checkout, fewer support calls, better data. Make each measurable and assign owners. |
| ARC-039-EX05 | 3 | Compare option A (mostly opex, subscription) and option B (mostly capex, build then run) in terms of cash profile and impact on the budget. |
| ARC-039-EX06 | 4 | Draft a one-page cost-benefit summary a finance partner can verify, listing the five assumptions that matter most. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Subscription, volume tiers, implementation partner, integration build, migration, environments, premium support, internal run team, security and compliance, training, data egress, contingency, exit costs; price uplift over time.
- **EX02** SaaS needs subscription, integration, configuration, support tier, and vendor management; custom needs build, infrastructure, upgrades, security, run team. Compare both all-in over the same period.
- **EX03** Cumulative: year 1 -2.4, year 2 -1.0, year 3 +0.4, so payback occurs in year 3 (about 2.7 years). Test: delay of benefits, adoption level, cost overrun, volume.
- **EX04** Conversion rate (owner: e-commerce head) with baseline; support calls per order (owner: customer service head); reporting time saved (owner: finance head); each with target and date, or marked non-financial.
- **EX05** A: lower upfront, higher ongoing cost, scales with usage, hits operating budget. B: large upfront spend, lower ongoing licence but higher run staff; capitalization rules decided by finance (confirm). The better option depends on the budget cycle and risk appetite.
- **EX06** Page includes: investment, five-year TCO, benefits with owners, ROI and payback, sensitivity, assumptions with sources; assumptions: volume growth, adoption, delivery delay, vendor uplift, staffing cost.

**Workplace evidence:** a cost model or business case you produced that was used in a funding decision.
