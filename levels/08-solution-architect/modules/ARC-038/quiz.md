# ARC-038 · Operational architecture — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 monitoring, alerting, and logging · O2 support models and ownership · O3 incident and change with vendors · O4 operational acceptance.

**Q01 (O1)** Operational design should be considered: A. After go-live B. During the first incident C. By the vendor only D. From the start of design
**Q02 (O1)** A SaaS component you cannot install agents in is best monitored with: A. Nothing B. Synthetic checks, integration error rates, and the vendor status feed C. Guesswork D. Customer complaints
**Q03 (O1)** A correlation ID helps support to: A. Follow one order across systems B. Reduce licence cost C. Encrypt data D. Choose a vendor
**Q04 (O1)** Good alerting: A. Alerts on every log line B. Never alerts C. Alerts on business-relevant symptoms with severity and routing D. Uses email only for all alerts
**Q05 (O2)** Support hours for store tills should follow: A. Office hours B. Developer preference C. The vendor's hours D. Store trading hours
**Q06 (O2)** An integration between two teams should have: A. Shared ownership B. No owner C. One named owner team and a named contact on the other side D. A vendor owner
**Q07 (O3)** Mapping internal severities to vendor priorities ensures: A. Fewer incidents B. A severity-1 incident gets the fastest vendor response without debate C. Lower cost D. No contracts
**Q08 (O3)** Vendor maintenance windows should: A. Feed the change calendar B. Be hidden C. Be ignored D. Be decided by stores
**Q09 (O4)** Operational acceptance criteria are best: A. Written at design time and verified before handover B. Written after go-live C. Optional D. Verbal
**Q10 (O4)** A runbook that nobody has walked through: A. Is acceptable B. Is a gap in operational acceptance C. Proves readiness D. Replaces monitoring

## Answers
Q01 D · Q02 B · Q03 A · Q04 C · Q05 D · Q06 C · Q07 B · Q08 A · Q09 A · Q10 B

## Practical part
Operating model review of the lab artifacts plus exercises `ARC-038-EX01` to `EX06`. Pass: quiz at least 8/10, at least five of six exercises correct, and a lab reviewed by a role-played head of IT operations with owners for every service and integration.
