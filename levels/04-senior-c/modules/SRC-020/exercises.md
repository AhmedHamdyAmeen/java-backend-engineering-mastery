# SRC-020 · Mentorship — Exercises and lab

## Lab
### SRC-020-LAB1 · Mentor a Mid learner
**Outcomes:** 1–4 · **Time:** ~3 h of your own work plus the four-week mentoring
Mentor a Level 3 learner through one capstone component over four weeks: agreement, delegation brief, coaching reviews, design feedback, and a growth plan. Keep a short log of sessions and decisions.
**Acceptance:** mentee feedback and their delivered result.

## Exercises (assessment: mentee feedback plus reflection)
| ID | Outcome | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRC-020-EX01 | 1 | Write a delegation brief for a mentee to add a retry policy to a payment client: outcome, boundaries, check-ins. |
| SRC-020-EX02 | 2 | Rewrite five blunt or fix-it review comments as coaching comments, labeled required or suggestion. |
| SRC-020-EX03 | 3 | Review a short design note with a missing failure-mode section; write design feedback on thinking habits. |
| SRC-020-EX04 | 4 | Write a growth plan with two focus areas against level expectations, including evidence and a stretch task. |
| SRC-020-EX05 | 1 | Case: the mentee is stuck and the deadline is near. List what you may do without taking over. |
| SRC-020-EX06 | 3 | Case: feedback so far has been only praise. Write a balanced feedback message on one design. |

## Solutions
- **EX01** Outcome: payment calls survive transient failure without duplicate charges; boundaries: owns the policy values, must ask before changing the idempotency scheme; check-ins weekly; review with tests.
- **EX02** For example: "What happens if two threads reach this?" (suggest) and "This risks double charge; see the idempotency principle" (required with reasoning).
- **EX03** Praise specific strengths, then note the habit: moving to solutions before listing failure modes; offer a checklist and ask the author to extend it.
- **EX04** Focus: design trade-offs and operability; evidence: decisions documented; stretch: lead a small design review; review date set.
- **EX05** Ask questions to unblock, share a similar example, pair for an hour on the hard concept, reduce scope; avoid doing it for them.
- **EX06** State a specific strength, then one concrete growth area with an example and a suggested next step.
- Outcome 2 is also covered by the lab log and Q04-Q05.

**Workplace evidence:** an engineer you mentored and how their scope grew.
