# SRC-020 · Mentorship — Quiz

Outcomes: O1 delegation · O2 coaching through reviews · O3 design feedback · O4 growth tracking.

**Q01 (O1)** Good delegation defines: A. Every keystroke B. Nothing C. Outcomes, boundaries, and support D. Only deadlines
**Q02 (O1)** Work for growth should be: A. Far beyond ability B. Slightly beyond current comfort with support C. Always trivial D. Only what the mentor dislikes
**Q03 (O1)** When a mentee struggles, a mentor should: A. Take over B. Leave them alone C. Blame them D. Unblock with questions and examples without taking over
**Q04 (O2)** In a coaching review, a comment should explain: A. The principle behind the point B. Only the replacement code C. Who is better D. Nothing
**Q05 (O2)** Why separate required from suggested comments? A. To make reviews longer B. To make clear what blocks merging and what is optional C. To avoid reviews D. To hide mistakes
**Q06 (O3)** Design feedback should also address: A. Font choice B. Only formatting C. Thinking habits such as considering alternatives and failure modes D. Team size
**Q07 (O3)** A good question for design feedback is: A. Why did you not copy mine? B. Who approved this? C. Is this finished? D. What would make you change this decision?
**Q08 (O4)** Growth is tracked against: A. Mentor's preferences B. Level expectations with evidence C. Hours worked D. Lines of code
**Q09 (O4)** A growth plan should have: A. Two or three focus areas B. Twenty goals C. No review date D. Only praise
**Q10 (O2)** Feedback that is only praise: A. Is ideal B. Always motivates C. Gives little guidance for improvement D. Is required

## Answers
Q01 C · Q02 B · Q03 D · Q04 A · Q05 B · Q06 C · Q07 D · Q08 B · Q09 A · Q10 C

## Practical part
Mentee feedback plus a written reflection (SRC-020 assessment) and the delivered component from `SRC-020-LAB1`. Pass: at least 8/10 on the quiz, mentee feedback that shows growth, and a reflection naming what you would do differently.
